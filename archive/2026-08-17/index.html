L1 Networks The Rollback

Harmony Plans Rollback Wiping 109,000 Transactions After ONE Exploit

Harmony will roll back its blockchain to 11:25 pm UTC on Aug. 11, discarding more than 109,000 transactions confirmed after its chosen checkpoint following an exploit that forged ONE tokens. The layer-1 network said validators would rebuild from replacement databases, arguing that selectively restoring transactions was unsafe because balances, contract states and nonces would differ on the replacement chain.

The discarded window includes 109,126 regular transactions and 315 staking transactions. Investigators have traced nearly all of the forged ONE to wallets or service boundaries and are working with exchanges, bridges and law enforcement. The move puts Harmony alongside Ravencoin among networks reversing already-confirmed activity after an exploit — ONE's market cap stood near $10.8 million.

DeFi The Oracle Attack

Stablecoin Balance Coin Crashes 99% After Reported $915K Exploit

Balance Coin, the algorithmic stablecoin of Balance Protocol, has fallen more than 99% to $0.001358 from $0.9954 after a reported exploit. Security firm SlowMist said an attacker manipulated an "abnormally low" Binance Bitcoin (BTCB) oracle price to liquidate collateral in multiple BTCB vaults that should not have been liquidatable, then swapped the extracted assets for profit — "a single-transaction combo" that exploited missing price protection and liquidation delay in the Maker-style system.

PeckShield put losses at $915,000 for 42DAO, the protocol's governance entity. Balance Coin is primarily backed by Bitcoin Cash, per the project's GitBook. The incident adds to a string of DeFi exploits this year as attackers continue to target smart contract flaws, compromised admin controls and bridge vulnerabilities.

Ethereum Post-Quantum Pivot

Ethereum Foundation Drops Poseidon From Post-Quantum Plan

The Ethereum Foundation is abandoning the Poseidon hash function in its planned post-quantum architecture, according to researcher Justin Drake, turning instead to established alternatives such as SHA or BLAKE. Poseidon had been earmarked for future post-quantum systems like leanVM, which would help Ethereum efficiently verify large volumes of blockchain activity — but advances in SNARKs mean conventional hashes can now perform efficiently enough to replace it.

Drake targets a production-ready leanVM for 2027, followed by deployments across Ethereum's consensus, data and execution layers in 2028. Eigen Labs founder Sreeram Kannan said established hash-based systems have fewer known avenues of attack and can deploy faster after years of scrutiny — joint work with the foundation and zero-knowledge firm Succinct has already increased proving speeds 2.5 times.

Institutions The Staking Wave

Fidelity Files With SEC to Add Staking to Ethereum ETF

Fidelity Investments plans to add staking to its spot Ether exchange-traded product, the Fidelity Ethereum Fund (FETH), according to a filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. Under normal conditions FETH could stake up to 100% of its Ether, excluding coins reserved for redemptions, expenses and liquidity — retaining 85% of rewards, paying 15% in staking fees, with quarterly cash distributions planned.

The filing follows Grayscale's October 2025 move to become the first US issuer with staking in spot crypto ETPs and BlackRock's separate iShares Staked Ethereum Trust. FETH had recorded about $2.13 billion in cumulative net inflows since its July 2024 launch and was leading pre-market gains across ETH funds, up 2.4%, ahead of Wednesday's US open.

✦ Feature: Fewer Winners in the Next Altseason ✦

Crypto's next altcoin season may produce fewer winners, market maker Wintermute warns in its H1 2026 OTC flow report: institutional counterparties generated 72% of spot flow across all tokens on its desk — the highest share on record, up from 61% in H2 2025. Liquidity is concentrating in the assets institutions favor, while activity across the market's "long tail" fades faster after price surges, suggesting future altcoin rallies could become narrower and more selective.

Between H1 2024 and H1 2026, the number of unique tokens traded by Wintermute's institutional counterparties grew just 24% versus 76% for retail clients. The pattern echoes across the market: the 10 largest non-stablecoin altcoins account for about 80.5% of the non-Bitcoin, non-stablecoin market cap, CryptoQuant CEO Ki Young Ju says the traditional rotation of Bitcoin profits into smaller assets has "basically disappeared," and Kaiko data shows the top 10 altcoins now claim 63% of altcoin trading volume. DWF Labs' Andrei Grachev argues broad rallies are giving way to selective sector moves — with institutions focused on Bitcoin, Ether and tokenized real-world assets.

Ethereum Hegotá — Scoping

Ethereum Devs to Narrow 66 Proposals Tied to Hegotá Upgrade

Ethereum developers are reviewing 66 proposals to scope the next major upgrade, Hegotá, with several aimed at bringing privacy into the protocol. FOCIL is currently the only EIP scheduled; contributors are pushing for Frame Transactions (EIP-8141), Keyed Nonces (EIP-8250) and Recent Roots (EIP-8272) to "unlock native privacy" without intermediaries. Core developers aim to ship Hegotá next year, with the next call set for Monday at 2:00 pm UTC.
