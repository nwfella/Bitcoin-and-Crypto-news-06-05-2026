Bitcoin Lows Pierce $63K as Asia Chip-Stock Crash Spreads to Wall Street

Bitcoin hit ten-day lows at Tuesday's Wall Street open as contagion from a major Asia stock-market correction spread to the US, with semiconductor-led losses sending South Korea's KOSPI down 10.8% in a single session. Crypto markets saw elevated long liquidations topping $510 million over 24 hours, with data from CoinGlass confirming the scale of the day's reversal. BTC/USD dipped below $63,000 for the first time since July 17. Semiconductor stocks are contending with intensifying scrutiny over hyperscaler capital expenditure — Alphabet posted its first cash burn on record at $5.9 billion — while Chinese AI models like Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 are raising questions about the return profile assumed by Western hyperscalers' massive spending commitments.

Lido Upgrade Aims to Slash Ethereum's Validator Count by One-Third

Lido, the liquid staking protocol, has launched an upgrade to its staking infrastructure that could reduce Ethereum's validator count by roughly one-third. The upgrade introduces Curated Module v2, which adds support for Ethereum's 0x02 withdrawal credentials, allowing validators to increase their effective balance from 32 ETH to as many as 2,048 ETH. The protocol projects the migration could cut Ethereum's validator set to about 628,000 from 880,000. The change is designed to trim Ethereum's consensus layer by reducing the number of validators and validator messages required to maintain the network, without affecting execution-layer activity that determines transaction fees and gas costs.

Security Hacks

Ethereum, Solana Led Crypto Hack Losses in H1 2026: Blockaid

Crypto losses topped $1 billion in the first half of 2026 as the industry recorded its highest number of hacks in a six-month period, according to Blockaid. Ethereum and Solana recorded the largest losses with roughly $332 million and $326 million in stolen funds respectively, out of 212 tracked security incidents. Code exploits drove Ethereum incidents, while compromised keys and signing infrastructure accounted for more than 98% of Solana's losses, largely driven by incidents linked to North Korea-linked cyber groups. The largest single exploit came from KelpDAO at $292 million, while the platform verified 3.4 times as many high-threshold exploits in H1 2026 as across all of 2025.
