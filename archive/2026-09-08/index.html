★ Sidechain Standoff — The Haul Comes Home ★

'White Hats' Return $270M in Bitcoin as Liquid Prepares to Restart

Purported white-hat hackers handed back 3,400 Bitcoin — roughly 85% of the $320 million snatched from the Liquid federation wallet on Sunday — after Blockstream confirmed patches for the compromised bridge. Roughly 598 BTC remains outstanding as federation members ready a coordinated restart.

By The Daily Cryptograph Staff | September 8, 2026

The great sidechain heist of Sunday has all but reversed itself. Onchain records show exactly 3,400 BTC — worth about $270 million — transferred back to the Liquid federation's wallet address, days after hackers withdrew roughly 4,000 BTC from the roughly 4,200 BTC that backs L-BTC, the network's wrapped Bitcoin. JAN3 chief executive and former Blockstream executive Samson Mow said Monday that the return followed confirmation from Blockstream that the affected bridge nodes had been patched, and that the company continues to engage the actors over the roughly 598 BTC still outstanding.

Blockstream said updated software had been deployed and federation members were preparing a coordinated restart of the paused network. Because Liquid issues L-BTC against Bitcoin held by its federation, the return of about 85% of the withdrawn Bitcoin restores much of the backing stripped away in the incident. The original withdrawal moved through SideSwap's Peg-out Authorization Key — which Liquid and SideSwap insist was not compromised — with the L-BTC traced instead to a bug in Elements, the open-source software underpinning the sidechain.

Treasuries Dividend Diplomacy

Strategy Skips Its Weekly Bitcoin Buy to Repurchase $176M of STRC Preferred Shares

Strategy, Michael Saylor's Nasdaq-listed Bitcoin treasury, skipped its weekly acquisition this week and instead repurchased 1.8 million shares of its preferred STRC stock for an aggregate $176.3 million between Aug. 31 and Sept. 7, according to a Tuesday filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. The company simultaneously doubled the size of its Digital Credit Securities Repurchase Program to $2 billion.

With no new coins bought, the treasury stands at 845,050 Bitcoin, acquired for a total of $63.6 billion at an average price of $75,412 apiece. The pause follows last week's $370 million purchase — Strategy's first since mid-June — and arrives as STRC trades at $97.70, a 2.3% discount to its $100 par value, a level that blunts the preferred stock's usefulness as a funding vehicle and may force the company to lean harder on the 12% annual dividend or on the Bitcoin-sale framework it unveiled June 29.

MSTR common shares were down more than 3% at last look in Tuesday trading even as STRC held roughly flat in premarket activity. With its preferred line trading beneath par, the world's largest corporate Bitcoin holder appears to be defending the very instrument that has bankrolled much of its accumulation — pausing fresh purchases while it shores up the shares.

Payments Stablecoin Shipping Lanes

Circle to Acquire Tazapay in $400M All-Stock Deal to Expand USDC Cross-Border Payments

Circle has agreed to acquire Tazapay, the Singapore-based cross-border payments platform, in a $400 million all-stock transaction expected to close in 2027, the stablecoin issuer announced Tuesday. Circle will pay in Class A common stock — the price subject to adjustments for Tazapay's debt, transaction expenses and cash, according to a US Securities and Exchange Commission filing — and the deal requires customary closing conditions plus approval from the Monetary Authority of Singapore.

Tazapay processes more than $25 billion in annualized payment volume and serves more than 60 banking and fintech partners with local payout rails covering more than 100 markets — up from $10 billion in annualized volume in August 2025. For Circle, the acquisition grafts a global settlement network onto USDC, the second-largest stablecoin, at a moment when cross-border payments have become the sector's most-promoted real-world use case.

The purchase folds payout infrastructure in-house rather than renting it, extending Circle's consolidation push as regulators in Singapore and beyond hammer out stablecoin frameworks — leaving the issuer in control of its own on- and off-ramps across more than a hundred markets.

✦ Feature: Protocol — Narrowing the Fork ✦

The Ethereum Foundation has ranked 62 proposed upgrades for its Hegotá fork and named two "must ship" headliners — EIP-7805 (FOCIL) on the consensus layer and EIP-8141 (Frame Transactions) on the execution layer — in the first unified view of the roadmap published by its Protocol cluster, drawing on input from roughly 60 researchers and engineers.

FOCIL is designed to give users a path to have eligible transactions included without relying on centralized block builders, strengthening Ethereum's resistance to transaction censorship. Frame Transactions would introduce native account abstraction and create a path toward post-quantum authentication — and, paired with two companion proposals, could also provide protocol-level building blocks for privacy applications.

The ranking marks the first time the Foundation's Protocol cluster has aired a single, unified view of proposed EIPs rather than the separate opinions of individual teams — and it signals which changes are expected to make the cut for Hegotá, and which remain merely under consideration, as the fork's scope firms up.

Treasuries The Five-Percent Solution

Bitmine Buys 28,000 More ETH, Completes 97% of Its 5%-of-Supply Treasury Goal

Bitmine Immersion Technologies, the world's largest publicly listed Ether treasury, bought another 28,086 ETH — about $69.5 million — last week, the company said in a Tuesday announcement, lifting its hoard to 5.93 million Ether acquired at an average price of $2,495 per coin. The purchase completes 97% of management's goal to accumulate 5% of the total Ether supply within 15 months.

Led by chairman Tom Lee, Bitmine has now extended its buying streak to 14 months; last week's 53,501-ETH acquisition pushed its holdings to 4.9% of Ethereum's 120.7 million circulating supply. The firm reports $15.7 billion in total assets, including 5.1 million in staked ETH expected to generate about $330 million in annualized staking revenue.

The accumulation continues against a punishing tape: Ether has fallen 16% since the start of 2026 and traded near $2,469 at 1:29 pm UTC Tuesday, leaving Bitmine nursing roughly $5.1 billion in unrealized losses on its position, according to Dropstab data — even as it drives toward the final 3% of its audacious goal.

Onchain Sixteen Years' Sleep

600 Bitcoin Mined in 2010 Stirs After 16 Years of Dormancy — No Satoshi Link, Says Whale Alert

Bitcoin mined in the spring of 2010 has stirred for the first time in more than 16 years: a dozen long-dormant addresses holding a combined 600 BTC — about $48 million — moved the coins on Saturday, according to onchain data reviewed by Cointelegraph.

Blockchain sleuth Whale Alert traced all 12 rewards to blocks mined in March 2010, when each block still paid the original 50 BTC subsidy — and found no connection to Satoshi Nakamoto, Bitcoin's pseudonymous creator, in any of them. The subsidy has since been halved four times, most recently in April 2024, when it fell from 6.25 BTC to today's 3.125 BTC per block.

The awakening of coins mined while Nakamoto was still active inevitably reignited speculation of a creator connection — speculation Whale Alert appears to have doused. "None of the blocks can be connected to Satoshi based on our research," a spokesperson told Cointelegraph. What moved the coins, and who holds them now, remains unknown.

Robinhood Chain on Track for $160M in Annual Fees by 2028: Bernstein

Bernstein analysts expect Robinhood's blockchain network to generate as much as $160 million in annual fees by 2028, powered by tokenized stock trading that has grown to about 27% of chain volume since its July 1 launch — while memecoin pairs have faded from 100% of activity to 36%. Uniswap AMM pools pairing memecoins with stock tokens are creating what the bank calls "reflexive demand" for both sides. In little more than two months, the chain leads all networks by daily fees at $2.13 million, per DefiLlama.

Memecoins A Token Born of a Laptop

Hunter Biden to Launch 'LAPTOP' Memecoin, With 20% Airdropped to TRUMP Holders

Hunter Biden plans to launch a memecoin called LAPTOP on Wednesday, earmarking 20% of its one-billion-token supply for holders of President Trump's TRUMP token, The Wall Street Journal reports. Biden and the project's co-founders will initially control 30%, locked for six months and fully vested over two years, with another 20% designated for charity and operating expenses. Up to 30% of supply could be burned if conditions are met — including a Democrat winning the 2028 election, Bitcoin reaching a record high, or LAPTOP's fully diluted valuation surpassing TRUMP's.

Policy The Yuan's Quiet Expansion

China Adds 8 Banks to Digital Yuan Network as Operator Count Hits 30

China's central bank has added eight lenders — including Ping An Bank, Bank of Shanghai and Bank of Hangzhou — to its digital yuan network, bringing the roster of e-CNY operating institutions to 30. The People's Bank of China expansion follows a 12-bank addition in April and is aimed at filling gaps in services for regional small and medium-sized enterprises and cross-border trade, said Merchants Union Consumer Finance chief researcher Dong Ximiao. Before this year, only 10 banks were authorized operators, with Industrial Bank the last to join in 2022.
