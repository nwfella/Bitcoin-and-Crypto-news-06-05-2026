ETF First Major Closure

Hashdex to Liquidate Its Spot Bitcoin ETF After Failing to Attract Assets

Hashdex is shutting down its spot Bitcoin ETF after failing to attract enough investor assets, marking the first major closure among the wave of US spot BTC funds. The asset manager said it will liquidate the fund this month after reviewing trading activity, operating costs and investor demand — remaining shareholders will receive cash distributions once the fund sells its approximately 225 Bitcoin holdings.

The ETF, which trades under the DEFI ticker on NYSE Arca, manages about $14.3 million in assets. It entered the US spot Bitcoin ETF market in March 2024 — months after rival products had already launched — and never grew beyond roughly $18 million, ranking as the smallest US-listed spot BTC fund and highlighting the growing divide between dominant issuers and smaller competitors.

Washington Crypto Adviser Exit

Bessent's Top Crypto Adviser Tyler Williams Exits US Treasury: Report

Tyler Williams, a senior US Treasury official overseeing digital asset policy and a key adviser to Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, has left the department, according to a Monday report by Punchbowl News. Bessent confirmed Williams' last day was Friday, saying he had been "instrumental" in advancing the administration's vision of making the US the "crypto capital of the world."

Williams joined Treasury in early 2025 after serving as head of policy at Galaxy Digital and is expected to return to the private sector. His departure comes as Congress remains deadlocked over the CLARITY Act ahead of the August recess — the bill requires 60 votes in the Senate, and bipartisan consensus has yet to emerge.

Tokenization Stablecoin Reserves

BlackRock Launches Tokenized Money Market Funds for Stablecoin Reserves

BlackRock, the world's largest asset manager, has launched two tokenized money market products designed to serve as reserve assets for stablecoins. The first — BSTBL — is a tokenized share class of its Select Treasury Based Liquidity Fund on Ethereum, with BNY serving as transfer agent. The second — BRSRV — is a newly created multi-chain fund for institutional investors that automatically reinvests daily dividends, with Securitize as transfer agent.

BlackRock said both funds are structured to qualify as eligible reserve assets for permitted US payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act, the federal stablecoin law enacted in July 2025. The launch expands BlackRock's footprint in the tokenized Treasury market, where its BUIDL fund remains the largest tokenized Treasury fund with more than $2.6 billion in assets.

Security Hardware Wallets

Coldcard's 5-Year Flaw Reveals Hardware Wallet Testing Gap: Kraken's Security Chief

Coldcard's five-year seed-generation flaw has exposed a broader weakness in how hardware wallets are independently tested, according to Kraken chief security officer Nick Percoco. In an X post Sunday, Percoco said the incident should be a "wake-up call" for hardware-wallet makers, calling for independent testing to verify that the approved source of randomness is the one actually used by production firmware.

"The payments industry does not let PIN entry devices ship without independent lab testing. The US government does not accept cryptographic modules without entropy source validation. Digital asset self-custody should not be the exception," he said. The comments follow an ongoing attack that has impacted over 4,500 addresses, draining nearly $90 million in Bitcoin.

Adoption Africa — Credentials

Kenya Moves 30 Million Academic Credentials Onto Avalanche Blockchain

Kenya has launched a blockchain-based system for verifying academic credentials through the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC), placing more than 30 million records on the Avalanche network in an effort to combat certificate fraud. The platform allows employers, universities and other institutions to verify credentials onchain, making records more difficult to tamper with.

"KNEC is bringing tamper-proof e-certification to Kenya on Avalanche," Avalanche said in a video announcing the initiative. For Avalanche, the rollout marks one of the blockchain's largest public-sector deployments — though AVAX was down 1.1% on Monday and remains roughly 69% below its level a year ago.

✦ Analysis: Is the Bear-Market Bottom Finally in Sight? ✦

Bitcoin could confirm a bear-market bottom in August with a monthly close above $63,000, according to 10x Research founder Markus Thielen, who said in a Monday report that July's close fell short of the threshold needed to confirm a technical bottom — but that a relatively small gain from July's closing level could trigger the reversal signal. The base case is that the Federal Reserve holds rates steady, though further increases in the 10-year Treasury yield could force a September hike, while the Iran conflict remains an unpredictable risk.

Supply-side pressure persists: miners could generate roughly 100,000 BTC of selling pressure as some shift businesses toward artificial intelligence, and Bitcoin treasury companies unwinding positions add further supply. But bottom-callers are stacking up — Grayscale's Zach Pandl says Bitcoin may have bottomed earlier than the traditional four-year cycle suggests, placing the low in September or October, while K33 notes that when more than half of supply is held at a loss, prior cycle bottoms followed within 13 to 31 days. With BTC still trading roughly 50% below its October peak above $126,000, the market waits for confirmation.
