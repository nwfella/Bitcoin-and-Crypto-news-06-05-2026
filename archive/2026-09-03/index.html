Stablecoins SoFiUSD Comes to Kraken

Kraken Parent Payward Partners With SoFi on Stablecoin, 24/7 Settlement

Payward, the parent of crypto exchange Kraken, has partnered with financial technology firm SoFi in a deal that brings SoFiUSD — the dollar-backed stablecoin issued by SoFi Bank — to Kraken and connects the exchange to SoFi's round-the-clock dollar settlement network. Under the arrangement, SoFi will use Kraken Prime as an additional source of digital asset liquidity, while Payward joins the SoFi Exchange Network (SEN) and gains access to SoFi's business banking services; qualified custody services could follow as the partnership matures.

For Kraken's institutional and business clients, membership in SEN unlocks continuous US dollar settlement across time zones — a feature the exchange has been assembling through a string of traditional-finance alliances. SoFiUSD launched in 2026 with reserves held in cash and short-term US Treasurys, and SoFi's 15.8 million members already trade crypto inside its app; routing those orders through Kraken Prime gives SoFi access to liquidity across multiple venues rather than a single order book.

The deal lands as Payward prepares for a public listing — a draft registration was confidentially submitted to the SEC in November 2025, though reports now push an IPO to the second quarter of 2027 at the earliest — and days after London Stock Exchange Group reportedly partnered with Payward to offer tokenized UK equities through its LSE 24 venue.

Payments Checkout Without the Bridge

Bybit Pay Integrates With Mesh to Expand Crypto Payments

Bybit Pay, the payments arm of the crypto exchange, has integrated with payments infrastructure provider Mesh, letting users pay and fund accounts on supported platforms directly from their Bybit balances — no withdrawals, no bridging, no first-party transfers. Merchants already integrated with Mesh can add Bybit Pay to their checkout through an existing connection, instantly reaching Bybit's claimed 80 million users, with programmable settlement options that let businesses control when and how payments settle across markets.

Mesh says its network now spans more than 300 wallets, exchanges and financial services platforms. The integration deepens a partnership forged in capital: in January, Mesh raised $75 million in a Dragonfly-led Series C at a $1 billion valuation, bringing total funding past $200 million, with plans to expand across Latin America, Asia and Europe.

ETF Flows Streaks Snap, Bitcoin Rebounds

Ether, XRP ETF Inflow Streaks End as Bitcoin Funds Rebound

The great ETF streak is over — at least for now. US-listed spot Ether ETFs recorded $48 million in net outflows on Wednesday, ending twelve consecutive trading days of inflows that had drawn $1.62 billion, per SoSoValue. BlackRock's ETHA led the redemptions at $53.4 million, with Fidelity's FETH losing $26.2 million and Grayscale's ETHE shedding $23.5 million, though BlackRock's staked ETHB partially offset the damage with roughly $53 million in inflows.

Spot XRP ETFs likewise posted $7.2 million in outflows, snapping an 11-session streak that had added about $170 million and lifted cumulative inflows to $1.68 billion. Bitcoin funds moved the other way, drawing $101.2 million a day after a $236.5 million bleed. The rotation proved a mere pause: by Thursday's session every major coin on the board was trading sharply higher, with Bitcoin reclaiming $81,000 and Ether climbing back above $2,500.

Tokenization Dubai's Regulatory Embrace

VARA, Securitize Sign MoU for Tokenization Innovation in Dubai

Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority and BlackRock-backed tokenization platform Securitize have signed a Memorandum of Understanding to advance tokenized assets and digital asset infrastructure across the emirate and the wider UAE. The framework will support regulated tokenization initiatives, foster institutional participation and explore how tokenized financial products should operate under Dubai's rulebook — a collaboration VARA's spokesperson calls "combining regulatory perspective with Securitize's experience in institutional tokenisation" rather than any single product or stack.

The accord lands as tokenization goes mainstream: total real-world asset holders have risen 103% in 30 days to 3.2 million, and the value of tokenized assets now stands at $38.5 billion, per RWA.xyz. Securitize ranks as the world's largest platform with $4.9 billion in tokenized assets under management, ahead of Ondo Finance's $3.5 billion. VARA, meanwhile, granted its 50th VASP license at the start of July — evidence, Securitize CEO Carlos Domingo says, that Dubai has become one of the world's most forward-looking jurisdictions for digital asset innovation.

✦ Feature: The Coldcard Trail ✦

The hacker behind the third wave of Coldcard wallet thefts has begun to move — swapping roughly 10% of the stolen Bitcoin for Ether through THORChain, the first onchain movement from any of the three hack waves, according to Galaxy Research's Alex Thorn. "The hacker appears to be having some issues swapping all the funds through THORChain — they keep getting refunded and he keeps retrying," Thorn wrote on X, noting analysts traced the funds to a fresh Ethereum address that he has shared with authorities and crypto companies.

The transfers follow an exploit that Galaxy has linked to the theft of at least 1,789 Bitcoin from 8,865 addresses — worth about $114.7 million at the time — with 87% of the haul unmoved as of last week. Blockchain security firm CertiK reported in August that hackers tied to the exploit had already sent 64 BTC and 200 ETH to mixers such as Tornado Cash. The fresh activity comes days after Thorn said the attackers remained active, citing the Aug. 28 sweep of a deliberately weakened researcher wallet designed to test their ability to find vulnerable keys. Whether the funds are headed for an exchange or further obfuscation, he said, remains unclear.

Hyperscale Data Ends Michigan BTC Mining as Holdings Fall 79%

Regulation Cert Petition Filed

New Jersey Asks Supreme Court to Settle Prediction Market Fight

New Jersey's Attorney General Jennifer Davenport has petitioned the US Supreme Court to decide whether states can regulate sports betting offered through CFTC-registered prediction markets such as Kalshi. The petition challenges an April Third Circuit ruling that favored Kalshi, and cites civil cases in "at least 20 states." "These companies have no right to offer their sports bets without following state law," Davenport said; Kalshi retorts it cannot be "regulated by 50 different regulators." A decision could determine federal-versus-state authority over the industry nationwide.

Prediction Markets Black Gold, No Expiry

Kalshi Seeks CFTC Approval for WTI Crude Perpetual Futures

Kalshi will reportedly seek regulatory approval for a West Texas Intermediate crude oil perpetual futures contract that never expires, filing with the CFTC as soon as next week, per Bloomberg. If approved it would be the first oil-linked perpetual to trade on a regulated US platform, trading 24 hours a day, five days a week. The push follows a June CFTC call for comments on 24/7 trading and energy-linked perps — and a July decision to halt a CME Group self-certified 24/7 crude contract while it reviewed the product.
