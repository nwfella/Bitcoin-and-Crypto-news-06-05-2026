Regulation The Press Conference

Trump Pushes for CLARITY Act Passage Alongside Crypto Leaders

US President Donald Trump continued to push for passage of a crypto market structure bill as the Senate remains in recess, holding a Wednesday press conference alongside Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong and Gemini co-founders Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in which he said lawmakers should pass "a fair version" of the CLARITY Act. Armstrong called the bill a chance to make US crypto policy "durable into the future, so it could survive for decades and decades to come," while Trump insisted the measure carries bipartisan support: "It's very bipartisan, I would say. Lot of Democrats support."

The president first urged CLARITY's passage in July following the death of Senator Lindsey Graham, whom Trump described as a "big supporter" of the bill. The Senate left for August recess without a vote; Senator Ruben Gallego weighed in at the Wyoming Blockchain Symposium in comments added after publication, even as the SEC advanced its own rulemaking for investment contracts involving crypto assets (see Feature below).

Institutions The Senior Notes

Ripple Raises $275 Million for US Prime Brokerage Expansion

Ripple raised $275 million in a senior note offering that closed Tuesday to fund its US expansion into financial services, with the senior unsecured notes issued in a private placement by its non-bank prime brokerage, Ripple Prime. President Noel Kimmel said the offering signals "confidence in our long-term vision for the growing intersection of traditional and digital asset financial infrastructure," with proceeds supporting prime brokerage, financing and multi-asset clearing.

The raise follows Ripple's roughly $1.25 billion acquisition of Hidden Road last year — which launched the institutional prime brokerage now branded Ripple Prime — and a $200 million credit facility from Neuberger Berman funds in May. Across Asia, South Korea's Jeonbuk Bank has separately signed on to use Ripple Payments for cross-border business settlement, the company's third partnership with a South Korean financial institution this year after Kyobo Life Insurance and Kbank.

Markets MiFID II

Kraken Launches US Stock Trading Across European Economic Area

Kraken has launched trading in more than 7,000 US-listed stocks for customers in the European Economic Area, expanding its traditional finance offering alongside cryptocurrencies and tokenized equities. Eligible EEA customers can trade US stocks through Kraken Pro and the mobile app under the exchange's Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II authorization, with the service provided by Payward Europe Digital Solutions, Kraken's Cyprus-based investment firm — commission-free, subject to applicable conditions.

The shares sit alongside more than 600 crypto assets and over 700 xStocks — tokenized versions of publicly listed equities — letting customers hold conventional shares and tokenized representations of the same assets on a single platform. Kraken launched xStocks in 2025; the product has since generated more than $38 billion in total transaction volume.

Treasuries The US Front

Metaplanet to Take Controlling Stake in Super League Enterprise

Metaplanet, the Tokyo-listed company that has adopted Bitcoin as its primary treasury reserve asset, plans to take a controlling stake in Nasdaq-listed Super League Enterprise — renamed Superplanet — as its US Bitcoin treasury vehicle. CEO Simon Gerovich said the company will contribute 2,100 BTC and $2.5 million in cash, roughly $135 million at current prices and just under 5% of Metaplanet's 43,000 BTC holdings.

The structure gives the group two avenues for raising capital — Superplanet tapping US markets while Metaplanet continues to raise in Japan — with either able to support the broader Bitcoin treasury strategy and US acquisitions. Super League Enterprise, which operates an immersive gaming, content and advertising business, saw its shares surge more than 50% on the announcement. The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter.

✦ Feature: From 'Inapt' Rules to a Fit-for-Purpose Framework ✦

The SEC's new regulatory proposal marks a significant step forward from "a set of inapt rules" toward "clear, sensible, enforceable" digital asset regulations, Commissioner Hester Peirce said — arguing that a "whole generation has struggled with the SEC's insistence" on applying rules designed for another era to crypto. Chairman Paul S. Atkins praised the initiative, saying the agency's prior enforcement-heavy approach "has driven investment offshore, limiting the type of protections that we can provide investors here."

The Tuesday proposal would create a "clear and fit-for-purpose framework for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets," allowing entities to raise capital while preserving investor protections. It lands days after the US Senate failed to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity (CLARITY) Act — and after Atkins told CNBC on July 27 that the SEC was "ready, willing, and able to come out with rules" if the Senate did not deliver legislation.

Accounting Cash Equivalents

FASB Proposes Conditions for Stablecoins as Cash Equivalents

The Financial Accounting Standards Board proposed guidance outlining when companies may classify certain stablecoins as cash equivalents under US GAAP. A qualifying digital asset would need an on-demand contractual redemption right, a direct redemption right with its issuer for a known cash amount and at least one-to-one segregated reserves held in short-term, high-quality assets — active secondary markets alone would not suffice, and reserves of crypto and gold would disqualify a token. Comments are open until Nov. 19.

Ethereum The Gas Model Shift

Ethereum Warns Developers Over Glamsterdam Gas Changes

The Ethereum Foundation warned that wallets, indexers and gas estimators relying on a hardcoded maximum gas limit "will break" under gas-model changes in the upcoming Glamsterdam upgrade, urging teams to test on the Plataberget public testnet, which launched Aug. 13. EIP-8037 introduces a separate state-gas dimension: a plain transfer to an existing account still costs 21,000 gas, but sending ETH to a new account would cost more. The fork activates on the network Thursday before the Sepolia and Hoodi testnets.
