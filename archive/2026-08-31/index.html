Ethereum The 65-Week Streak

Bitmine Extends Ether Buying Streak to 65 Weeks, Now Holds 4.9% of Supply

Bitmine Immersion Technologies extended its Ether buying streak to 65 consecutive weeks, adding 53,501 ETH last week as a broader crypto market recovery lifted the value of its stack. The purchase brought Bitmine's holdings to more than 5.9 million ETH, valued at roughly $14.8 billion based on an Ether price of $2,511 as of Sunday — approximately 4.9% of Ethereum's total supply.

Chairman Tom Lee said Ether, Bitcoin and Solana have been the three best-performing major assets since June 30, with ETH leading gains: "We believe this sets the stage for institutions to add to their crypto holdings given the substantial outperformance of crypto versus other macro assets in 3Q so far." The company still sits on roughly $5.1 billion in unrealized losses on its Ether holdings, per DropsTab data. NYSE-traded BMNR was up 1.3% at $24.09 Monday morning, poised to end the month almost 40% higher.

Treasuries The Fifth-Largest Holder

Strive Buys 1,800 Bitcoin for $143M, Becomes Fifth-Biggest Corporate Holder

Strive, the publicly traded asset manager and Bitcoin treasury company, added 1,800 BTC to its holdings last week, paying approximately $143 million — an average of $79,431 per Bitcoin including fees and expenses — for purchases executed between Aug. 24 and Aug. 28.

The acquisition lifted Strive's total holdings to 23,156 Bitcoin, up from 21,356 a week earlier, and pushed it past crypto exchange Bullish to become the fifth-largest publicly traded corporate BTC holder. The purchases have coincided with the broad market recovery that began Aug. 19, when the US Treasury Department announced its latest policy shift. Strive isn't alone in ramping up: Michael Saylor's Strategy announced Monday it had resumed buying, lifting its own holdings back above 845,000 BTC following four sales since May.

Network The Chain Halt

Cronos Halts Network After Tectonic Exploit Involving Estimated $75M

Cronos halted its blockchain after an exploit targeting decentralized lending protocol Tectonic involved an estimated $75 million, most of which remains on the network. On Sunday, the chain identified the exploit and paused operations, with Tectonic separately warning users not to interact with the protocol.

Researcher Weilin Li said the attacker exploited TONIC's 20% collateral factor and thin liquidity, pumping the governance token's price 100-fold within 20 minutes. Li initially estimated $66 million was affected, and said the attacker bridged about $6 million to Ethereum before the halt, leaving $60 million on Cronos. Crypto.com CEO Kris Marszalek said the exchange's app and platform were unaffected and operating normally. Cronos and Tectonic have not said whether they will restrict the attacker's addresses, recover assets or compensate users.

Treasuries The Coinbase Transfer

Metaplanet Moves $377M in Bitcoin to Coinbase, Fueling Sale Speculation

Metaplanet, the fourth-largest corporate Bitcoin treasury, moved 4,800 BTC worth nearly $377 million to Coinbase Prime, triggering speculation that the Japanese company may be selling. According to Arkham Intelligence data, the transfer follows a 2,000 BTC move worth $155.5 million two days earlier, bringing weekly transfers to 10,270 BTC.

Blockchain-data profile Onchain Lens suggested the Bitcoin was moved "likely to sell," though other observers cautioned the transfers could be collateral moves. Metaplanet holds 35,102 BTC worth $2.75 billion; a confirmed sale would reduce its position substantially. The news comes as French treasury company Capital B moves in the opposite direction, announcing it raised €21 million for its own Bitcoin accumulation.

✦ Feature: The Buyback Boom ✦

Crypto projects have spent a record $638 million repurchasing their own tokens so far in 2026 — with Hyperliquid and Pump.fun alone accounting for nearly 90% of the total. The figure is up from $545 million during the same period last year and a paltry $366,000 in 2023, according to Allium Labs data cited by the Financial Times.

Token buybacks — the crypto analogue of corporate share repurchases — remain rare in the industry, but more projects are adopting them. The Hyperliquid (HYPE) and Pump.fun (PUMP) tokens have rewarded the strategy with massive outperformance: HYPE is up 145% and PUMP up 109% year-to-date, while Bitcoin is down 10% and total crypto market capitalization has declined 11.9% over the same period. On Thursday, the Ethena Foundation opened a vote on a fee-switch proposal that would direct 95% of net revenue from core business lines toward purchasing ENA — a sign the playbook is spreading.

Politics The Pay-to-Play Ban

California Advances Bill to Ban Memecoin Issuance by Public Officials

California lawmakers passed Assembly Bill 2409, restricting public officials' involvement with memecoins over conflicts-of-interest and "pay-to-play" concerns. The Senate voted 40-0 and the Assembly concurred 78-0. If enacted, digital asset service providers would be barred from offering California residents memecoins issued on or after Jan. 1, 2027 by or in partnership with federal officials — a direct response to the TRUMP token, whose buyers are an estimated $3.2 billion underwater.

Stablecoins The 2027 Filing Break

Japanese Regulator Requests Tax Exemption for Trust-Type Stablecoins

Japan's Financial Services Agency submitted a request to exempt trust-type stablecoins from mandatory beneficiary-by-beneficiary tax filings starting in fiscal year 2027. The FSA argued the tokens circulate among a broad user base, are used in frequent transactions, and holders earn no income. Subject to legislative approval, the exemption could apply from April 1, 2027 — the start of Japan's fiscal year.

Gaming The 1:1 Promise

The Sandbox Pledges 1:1 Repayment After $700K Bridge Exploit

The Sandbox will repay eligible SAND holders 1:1 after an Aug. 21 exploit drained 14.7 million tokens — worth about $700,000 — from an Ethereum bridge vault. Legitimate bridged-SAND holders on Base or BNB Smart Chain will receive an equal amount of Ether within a claims window opening in two weeks. The attacker exploited a configuration flaw allowing them to become the sole verifier of incoming bridge messages.
