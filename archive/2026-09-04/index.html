Markets The Yen Trade

Bitcoin Reclaims $80,000 as Weak Dollar Meets Suspected Yen Intervention

Bitcoin rallied about 5% during US trading hours to reach $81,000, near the highs of last month's surprise upside, as the Japanese yen strengthened in what traders widely suspect is central bank intervention. After USD/JPY dropped to 158.5 on Wednesday, the pair slid further to 155.4 — a near-2.5% move in 24 hours that analyst account The Macro Paper called impossible "without any major intervention" — putting pressure on the dollar index, which fell to 99. Downside in the DXY has historically been a tailwind for Bitcoin.

The move revived memories of the Q3 2024 carry-trade unwind: Polymarket odds of a Bank of Japan rate hold collapsed from 12% to 1% on Wednesday, and markets now price a 98% probability of a 25-basis-point hike at the BOJ's Sept. 18 meeting. Arthur Hayes, CIO of Maelstrom, sees the currency defense as liquidity-positive, arguing that the FIMA repo facility can supply Japan dollar liquidity against Treasury collateral.

Shares of Michael Saylor's Strategy rose 8.6% on the day and sit up 70% from their late-June lows, though still roughly 10% lower year-to-date; its variable-rate perpetual preferred stock STRC — frequently compared to a money-market fund — remains stuck below its $100 par value at $97.80.

Enforcement Transatlantic Pact

US, UK Launch Joint Alliance Targeting Crypto Scam Centers

The United States and United Kingdom have formed a first-of-its-kind law enforcement alliance against the scam centers behind crypto and cyber-enabled investment fraud. On Thursday, the US Attorney's Office for the District of Columbia, the Crown Prosecution Service of England and Wales and the UK's National Crime Agency signed a memorandum of understanding to run parallel investigations into common targets, share intelligence on organized crime syndicates and coordinate which jurisdiction prosecutes each case. The DOJ says the authorities have already identified overlapping cases and plan an in-person disruption operation with private-sector partners in London in early October.

The pact extends the Scam Center Strike Force launched in November 2025 to dismantle Chinese organized crime networks operating compounds primarily in Southeast Asia. It lands as reported US losses from crypto investment fraud jumped 89% to $8.65 billion in 2025 — and on the same day the Treasury's FinCEN published an analysis tying roughly $12.7 billion in crypto transactions between September 2023 and December 2025 to overseas scam centers, calling digital asset investment scams "one of the most significant fraud threats facing Americans today."

Banking Two Charters, Crypto Plans

Revolut, OpenReserve Win Preliminary US Approval for Crypto-Focused National Banks

Revolut and Andreessen Horowitz-backed OpenReserve have received preliminary conditional approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to charter national banks — Revolut in Connecticut and OpenReserve in Utah — with both planning cryptocurrency and stablecoin services, according to decisions published Wednesday. The OCC said Revolut wants its own US bank to provide services at lower cost and with greater efficiency than its current model, which relies on FDIC-insured partner banks.

Revolut plans digital asset custody and would let customers use crypto assets, including stablecoins, for cross-border transfers, alongside Revolut-branded stablecoins issued by a third party. OpenReserve — founded in 2025 by MoneyLion founder and former CEO Dee Choubey — is building a blockchain-based bank offering traditional banking services alongside tokenized deposits, digital asset custody and, through a future subsidiary, US dollar-backed stablecoins. Neither bank can open until it satisfies the OCC's preopening requirements and secures final approval.

Tokenization Treasury Titans on Chain

Bitfinex Securities Lists Tokenized Notes Tied to Strategy and Metaplanet

Bitfinex Securities, the regulated tokenized investment platform associated with the Bitfinex exchange, has listed five tokenized notes giving eligible investors exposure to the shares of Strategy, Metaplanet, Sweden's H100 Group and France's Capital B — plus Strategy's variable-rate perpetual preferred stock, STRC. The platform describes the launch as the first time such products have been made available for secondary trading on a regulated tokenized securities exchange.

Issued through ORO (II), a Luxembourg umbrella securitization fund managed by SICOS Securities, the notes are backed by the underlying securities — held with regulated financial institutions — but do not convey direct share ownership. Fractional exposure starts at about $1, trading against the US dollar, USDt and Bitcoin, though availability excludes US persons. The listings follow a record $50 million tokenized capital raise for metals company Alkemya in August; the platform says its listed assets now exceed $500 million.

✦ Feature: The IMF's Bitcoin Reckoning ✦

El Salvador used no public funds to accumulate Bitcoin after the first review of its $1.4 billion IMF program — the lender said Thursday, attributing the growth of the country's holdings to private donations and revealing that majority ownership and operational control of the Chivo wallet has been transferred to a private operator, with the government retaining a minority stake and custodial responsibilities.

The statement answers how El Salvador's stash grew after the country reported acquiring 1,090 BTC worth $100 million in November 2025, renewing questions about compliance with the December 2024 agreement that limited public-sector Bitcoin involvement and barred "voluntary accumulation." President Nayib Bukele had insisted the purchases were "not stopping" and pledged at least one BTC per day. Documents supplied by Salvadoran authorities verified the donations, the IMF said, and no further accumulation beyond them is expected.

Security Lending Protocol Hit

Cronos Halts Network After Estimated $75M Tectonic Exploit

Cronos halted its blockchain after an exploit of decentralized lending protocol Tectonic involved an estimated $75 million, most of it still on the network. Researcher Weilin Li described a "Mango-market style" pump-and-borrow attack exploiting TONIC's 20% collateral factor and thin liquidity — the attacker pumped the governance token 100-fold in 20 minutes before borrowing other assets, bridging about $6 million to Ethereum before the halt. Crypto.com CEO Kris Marszalek said the exchange and app were unaffected; no restart timeline has been announced.

Ethereum EEZ Rollup Approved

GnosisDAO Approves Gnosis Chain's Transition to an Ethereum Economic Zone

GnosisDAO approved GIP-153, moving Gnosis Chain from a standalone layer-1 to a ZK-proven Ethereum Economic Zone rollup — 123,158 GNO in favor across 54 voters, well past the 75,000 quorum. The validator set will be retired and the network will settle on Ethereum, with an initial launch targeted for late 2026 or early 2027. The shift would let Gnosis-native smart contracts call Ethereum and use the result in the same transaction, unlocking mainnet assets and liquidity.

Core Excess Rewards Fork

Core DAO Plans Emergency Hard Fork After Excess Validator Rewards

Core DAO is coordinating an emergency hard fork after validators claimed more CORE rewards than the blockchain intended to issue. The team says the incident is contained — "malicious validators" can no longer draw excess rewards — and the fork will be a forward upgrade with no rollback of confirmed transactions. Several exchanges paused CORE transfers during the investigation, including Coinbase, Bithumb and Bitget. Core has yet to disclose how much was issued or publish its technical postmortem.

Onchain First Movement

Coldcard Hacker Swaps Stolen Bitcoin for ETH via THORChain

A hacker tied to the third wave of Coldcard wallet thefts has begun swapping stolen Bitcoin for Ether through THORChain — the first onchain movement from any of the three waves, per Galaxy Research's Alex Thorn, who says the attacker moved about 10% of the funds. "They keep getting refunded and he keeps retrying," Thorn noted, as analysts traced the assets to a fresh Ethereum address shared with authorities. The exploit is linked to at least 1,789 BTC stolen from 8,865 addresses.
