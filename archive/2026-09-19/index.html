Coinbase Asks Washington for Single-Stock Perpetuals

A filing with the CFTC would put never-expiring futures on roughly fifty to sixty American equities — Apple, Microsoft, Tesla and Nvidia among them — as Binance opens a currency desk that never closes and Bastion takes a federal trust charter.

By The Daily Cryptograph Staff | September 19, 2026

The Coinbase has filed with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission to list single-stock perpetual futures in the United States, seeking to carry a derivatives product long native to crypto markets into individual American equities. The filing, submitted Friday through Coinbase Derivatives, would give US traders 24/5 exposure to individual stocks without owning the underlying shares — contracts that, unlike traditional futures, do not expire. The Wall Street Journal reported Friday that Coinbase intends to begin with perpetuals tied to roughly 50 to 60 stocks, Apple, Microsoft, Tesla and Nvidia among them.

The contracts are classified by the CFTC as single-stock futures and remain pending approval. The step was preceded by another: on Sept. 1, Coinbase Derivatives filed a Form 1-N with the Securities and Exchange Commission to register as a national securities exchange for the purpose of offering security futures. Coinbase already sells stock perpetuals to eligible traders outside the United States, having launched the product in March with contracts tracking major US stocks and indexes. At the time, the company said the products were unavailable to US persons.

The mirror image arrived the same week from Binance, which said it will launch 24/7 foreign-exchange perpetual futures beginning Monday with a US dollar–Brazilian real contract settling in USDT at up to 100x leverage. Traditional FX closes for the weekend; Binance will instead run a dual-mode pricing system — a weighted index drawn from third-party data providers during regular hours, and an orderbook-based mechanism using an exponentially weighted moving average on weekends and holidays. Trading head Shunyet Jan framed it as price discovery beyond banking hours. Bybit introduced 24/7 EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY perps less than two weeks earlier; Kraken has offered FX perps since April 2025. Global OTC FX turnover averaged $9.6 trillion a day in April 2025, per the Bank for International Settlements.

On the banking side of the same convergence, stablecoin infrastructure provider Bastion said the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency granted it preliminary conditional approval for a US trust bank charter, adding federal supervision to the state licences it already holds. Licensed as Bastion Platforms National Trust Company, it will offer stablecoin custody, wallets, payment infrastructure and white-label issuance from a single federally regulated entity — though it could not accept deposits or make loans, separating it from a conventional commercial bank. Ripple holds conditional approval for a similar charter; Circle and BitGo have final approval; Kraken parent Payward, Zerohash and Block have applied. Thirteen coins on our tape, twelve green: the price of a token has rarely mattered less to the people now building the rails it settles on.

Crypto Stocks Erase the CLARITY Selloff in a Single Session

Markets September 19, 2026

Crypto-linked equities rebounded sharply on Friday, reversing the losses that followed the Senate's Sept. 15 failure to advance the CLARITY Act, as Bitcoin climbed back above $80,000. Strategy led the gains, rising more than 13%, while Coinbase and American Bitcoin climbed about 11% each and Robinhood gained nearly 9%, according to Yahoo Finance data. Circle, Strive and miner Riot Platforms added between roughly 5% and 7%.

The turn came because regulators stopped waiting for Congress. On Thursday the CFTC issued a no-action position for providers of passive software, while the SEC announced temporary exemptions for certain platforms facilitating onchain trading of tokenized securities. The same week, the CFTC sent a crypto market rulemaking titled "Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions and Regulation Crypto Asset Markets" to the White House for review, the filing landing at the early "prerule" stage.

Stablecoins Get a Federal Charter — With Strings

Banking September 19, 2026

Bastion said the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency granted it preliminary conditional approval for a US trust bank charter, adding federal supervision to the state licences it already holds, according to a news release on Friday. The proposed bank could not accept deposits or make loans — the line that separates it from a conventional commercial bank — but as Bastion Platforms National Trust Company it would offer stablecoin custody and wallets, payment infrastructure and white-label issuance from one federally regulated entity.

"Stablecoins have moved from emerging technology into core financial infrastructure, and that requires a different standard of trust, governance and regulatory rigor," said CEO Nassim Eddequiouaq. The company has been building toward federal supervision since acquiring its New York trust charter in February 2025, and Cointelegraph reported in September 2025 that it had raised $14.6 million in a round led by Coinbase Ventures with Sony, Samsung's investment arm, a16z and Hashed participating.

The Currency Desk That Never Closes

Derivatives September 19, 2026

Binance is expanding into foreign exchange with 24/7 perpetual futures, starting Monday with a USDBRLUSDT contract that settles in USDT and offers up to 100x leverage. The design answers an old problem: FX closes at the weekend, and crypto does not. During regular trading hours Binance will track a weighted index from third-party data providers; on weekends and public holidays it will use an orderbook-based mechanism built from an exponentially weighted moving average of prices rather than external feeds.

The launch lands less than two weeks after Bybit introduced 24/7 perpetuals tracking EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY, also USDT-settled at up to 100x. Kraken entered earlier, launching FX perpetuals on the euro, pound, Australian dollar, yen and Swiss franc in April 2025 with up to 50x leverage — the exchange had offered spot FX since 2020 and reported $5.7 billion in FX spot volume in the first part of 2025. The prize is the largest market in finance: global OTC FX turnover averaged $9.6 trillion a day in April 2025, according to the Bank for International Settlements.

Zcash Tops $20 Billion at Its Highest Price Since 2016

Privacy September 19, 2026

Zcash climbed to its highest price since 2016, extending a rally that pushed the privacy-focused cryptocurrency's market capitalisation above $20 billion — roughly 45% higher over the past week and 138% over thirty days. ✦ "A Buyer That Stopped Buying Is Not Support" — The $80.5K Ceiling ✦

Bitcoin is no longer a target for corporate treasuries, and the ones already holding are underwater. Onchain analytics platform Glassnode reports that in 2026 listed companies bought about 5,900 BTC — less than 7% of their purchases in July 2025 alone, when companies absorbed 89,000 BTC with BTC/USD above $100,000. The group's average entry, the Corporate Treasury Cost Basis, sits at $80.5K, roughly 6% above spot. "Their entry sits at $80.5K... so the group as a whole is under water," Glassnode wrote in The Week Onchain.

Two attempts to reclaim that level in 2026 both failed, price unable to hold above it. Glassnode is blunt about what a stalled treasury bid means for the tape: "A buyer that has stopped buying and holds a paper loss is not support." Its conclusion is a "market in waiting" as fresh investor capital cools into the second half of September. The largest holder illustrates the point — Strategy made its most recent purchase at the end of August, adding 4,603 BTC in its first acquisition in two months, and the cost basis on its 845,050 BTC now stands at $75,412. "A reclaim of $80.5K would put the treasuries back in profit and remove one layer of overhead supply," Glassnode wrote. "Until then, their entry is one more ceiling."

Four Years for the Banker Who Took Bribes in Bitcoin

Justice

A former official at China Construction Bank (Asia) who falsely authenticated letters of credit worth more than $1.6 billion was sentenced to four years in prison and ordered to make restitution of more than $470,000 in cryptocurrency bribes. Lam Chun-yin, 32, a customer relationship manager, had pleaded guilty in Hong Kong's District Court, The Standard reported Saturday. Judge Ernest Lin Kam-hung said deterrent sentences were necessary even for first-time offenders, and that banking and insurance are the backbone of Hong Kong's economy — the scheme had undermined its standing as a global financial hub. A Fourth Crossover, and Three for Three Behind It

Analysis

Bitcoin is showing signs that its latest long-term floor is already in, according to a classic trading indicator. Analyst Willy Woo flagged readings from the Fisher Transform — a trend tool created in 2002 that smooths price action with a log-based transform — showing that on monthly time frames, a sharp upward reversal in which its two trend lines cross has corresponded to bear-market bottoms. The cross occurred in July at −2.26. "BTC bottoms: 3 for 3 without fake out. Latest cross is the 4th on record," Woo wrote. He concedes price could still consolidate and continue lower, noting that in bull markets the same indicator produces false bearish crosses because speculative traders are present — while at bottoms they are largely absent.

Onchain Malware Up 420% as State Hackers Move In

Security

North Korean and Iran-linked hackers were responsible for the majority of a 420% increase this year in malware stored on public blockchains, according to a Chainalysis report. State-linked actors accounted for roughly two-thirds of new activity, in which attackers park malware instructions or infrastructure pointers in chain data — raising durability, because the information survives the takedown of domains, servers or repositories. Brussels Recruits Merchants for a Digital Euro

CBDC

The European Central Bank is recruiting e-commerce and mobile-commerce merchants in the euro area to test beta digital euro payments during a 12-month pilot expected to begin in the second half of 2027. Applications close Oct. 27; participation is voluntary and unpaid, and successful applicants must establish or adapt a relationship with a participating payment service provider. The beta will sit close to the currency described in draft legislation, but without legal tender status. In July the ECB selected 36 payment service providers from more than 50 applicants, including Revolut, Stripe, Deutsche Bank and UniCredit. A final decision on issuing the currency will come only after EU legislation is adopted.

The Exploit That Got Exploited First

Security

An attacker exploited a custom module attached to an Ethereum Safe wallet to extract roughly $7.7 million in rsETH — and was front-run before taking control of the funds. Per blockchain security firm Blockaid, the attacker used a public keeper multicall to steer a custom Uniswap v4 liquidity module into an attacker-created hooked pool, where aEthrsETH was unwrapped into rsETH. An MEV bot known as Yoink captured the rsETH first, transferring about 18.93 ETH (roughly $46,000) to an address labelled as a block builder in the same transaction. Kelp, the protocol behind rsETH, placed the receiving address under a 24-hour pause as a precautionary, wallet-level measure, saying its contracts were unaffected and rsETH remains fully backed.
