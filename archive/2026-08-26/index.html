Markets The Seven-Day Streak

Spot Bitcoin ETFs Add $314M as August Inflows Top $3B

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds extended their inflow streak to seven trading days on Tuesday, attracting $314.37 million in net inflows and pushing August's haul to $3.03 billion — leaving the funds just $390 million short of October 2025's monthly total with four trading sessions remaining, according to SoSoValue data.

The rebound has cut year-to-date net outflows by more than half to $2.26 billion, with total net assets reaching $99.05 billion and cumulative inflows since launch at $54.36 billion. Bitcoin briefly surged past $80,000 on Tuesday before settling at $78,880, down 2% on the day, while the Crypto Fear & Greed Index eased to 65 from 74 — still in "Greed" territory. US spot Ether ETFs also extended their streak to seven days, adding $179.8 million and bringing the period's inflows to about $1 billion.

Stablecoins The EURR Debut

Revolut Launches EURR Euro Stablecoin in Europe

Revolut has begun rolling out its first stablecoin — a euro-pegged token called EURR — to selected customers in Denmark, Poland and Portugal, with a phased expansion to other European Economic Area markets planned later this year. The token is issued by Bridge Building S.A., the Luxembourg entity of Stripe-owned stablecoin infrastructure company Bridge.

The MiCA-compliant launch, covering roughly 2 million customers in the first wave, arrives as Revolut withdraws Tether's USDt from the EEA and Switzerland, converting remaining balances after Aug. 31. EURR will initially launch on Ethereum with external wallet transfers available immediately for select customers, backed by reserves held and managed by Bridge under EU rules; Revolut said fiat transactions will carry no fees or spreads and that tokens denominated in other currencies are in development.

Ethereum The 5% March

Bitmine ETH Holdings Near 5% as Ether Breaks $2,500

Tom Lee's Bitmine Immersion Technologies bought 32,447 Ether last week, bringing its holdings to 5,847,611 ETH — roughly 4.8% of Ethereum's circulating supply — and extending a weekly buying streak that has run about 14 months since its treasury strategy launched on June 30, 2025. The company is now 97% of the way toward its stated goal of owning 5% of the supply, with 5.07 million ETH staked.

The purchases coincided with a sharp rally: Ether has gained more than 32% since last Wednesday, when the US Treasury announced plans to double monthly buybacks of certain long-dated bonds, breaking above $2,500 for the first time since January. The recovery has narrowed Bitmine's paper losses from more than $8.4 billion a week ago to below $5 billion on a $19.5 billion investment, while the company's NYSE shares rose about 8.7% to open the week.

Markets The $1.5B Raise

Kalshi Discloses $1.5B Equity Offering in SEC Filing

Prediction market operator Kalshi disclosed that $1.12 billion has been sold under a nearly $1.5 billion equity offering that began April 3, according to a Form D filed Tuesday with the SEC listing 71 investors — leaving about $380 million to be sold. The company confirmed the filing relates to its Series F round at a $22 billion valuation, announced in early May.

The offering relies on Rule 506(b) of Regulation D, an exemption allowing unregistered sales subject to certain requirements. The filing lands as Kalshi faces legal challenges from US states over its offerings: in mid-August, a Washington state judge ordered the platform to stop offering a broad range of event contracts there, rejecting its argument that federal law overrides state gambling laws.

✦ Feature: The BankChain Alliance ✦

39 US state banking associations join forces for an industry-owned nationwide blockchain network, targeting 2027. The newly formed BankChain Alliance says its network will support smart payment tools, tokenized deposits, stablecoins and automated settlement, and plans to be interoperable with other blockchains — inviting banks nationwide to take ownership stakes, though governance and funding details remain undisclosed. The participating associations represent thousands of financial institutions.

BankChain joins a wave of US bank-led networks since late 2025: The Clearing House's onchain money initiative backed by JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citi, BNY and Wells Fargo; the regional Cari network from Huntington, First Horizon, M&T, KeyBank and Old National, which had attracted 30-plus banks by July; the community-bank DTX Consortium; and Open Standard's Open USD stablecoin consortium with 140-plus companies. Unlike independently issued stablecoins, tokenized deposits remain claims on individual banks — commercial bank money that stays on bank balance sheets while enabling programmable, round-the-clock transfers.

Ethereum The EEZ Rollup

GnosisDAO Approves Gnosis Chain for Ethereum Economic Zone

GnosisDAO voted to retire Gnosis Chain's validator set and transition the network into a ZK-proven Ethereum Economic Zone (EEZ) rollup that settles directly on Ethereum — GIP-153 passed with 123,158 GNO in support against 115 opposed, far exceeding the 75,000 quorum. An initial launch is targeted for late 2026 or early 2027, making Gnosis Chain the EEZ's first deployed instance while retaining its applications, balances and xDAI gas token.

Japan The Settlement Fast Lane

Japan Weighs Tokenized BOJ Deposits for Faster Settlement

Japan's FSA, Ministry of Finance, Bank of Japan and financial institutions plan to establish a study group this summer on a blockchain system for immediate, around-the-clock cash settlement of stock and Japanese government bond trades. The proposal would convert some bank deposits held in BOJ current accounts into digital tokens circulating onchain for interbank settlement, with a development plan expected early in 2027 and operations potentially starting in the early 2030s — versus today's T+2 equity and T+1 JGB settlement.

Storm's lawyers requested the delay to prepare after the court rules on the motion; prosecutors opposed it. Ethereum The Privacy Upgrade

Ethereum Devs to Narrow 66 Proposals tied to 2027 Hegotá Upgrade

Ethereum developers are reviewing 66 proposals as they scope the next major upgrade, Hegotá, with several aimed at bringing native privacy into the protocol. FOCIL — fork-choice enforced inclusion lists boosting censorship resistance — is the only EIP confirmed for inclusion, with Frame Transactions (EIP-8141), Keyed Nonces (EIP-8250) and Recent Roots (EIP-8272) expected to join so privacy apps can work without intermediaries; core devs aim to ship the upgrade next year.
