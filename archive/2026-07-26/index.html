Exchanges Shutdown

BitMart to Wind Down Exchange, End Trading by Aug. 26

BitMart has announced it will wind down its cryptocurrency exchange, ending all trading services on Aug. 26 before ceasing operations entirely on Jan. 31, 2027. "After a careful evaluation of the Company's operating conditions, market environment, and future strategic direction, BitMart has made the difficult decision to commence an orderly wind-down of its trading platform operations," it said in a Sunday notice. Under the shutdown plan, BitMart has stopped accepting new user registrations and deposits, while futures trading has entered reduce-only mode and spot markets no longer accept new orders. BitMart joins a growing list of crypto trading platforms that have announced plans to close shop in recent months, including BitMEX and Dango — both of which said this week they would shut down their respective platforms.

Exchanges Security

Binance 'Red Teams' Its Own Staff Every Month to Keep Hackers Out

Cryptocurrency exchange Binance runs simulated phishing attacks against its own employees and can fire staff who repeatedly fail the tests, according to Binance chief security officer Jimmy Su. The fake attacks are conducted by Binance's red team, an internal ethical hacking unit whose job is to break into systems to identify vulnerabilities. "We do phishing attacks on our own employees on a monthly basis just so we understand if our security hygiene is improving," Su told Cointelegraph. "The ones that have failed it, we will do remediation training." The measure shows the lengths crypto companies will go to prepare for social engineering attacks, which AMLBot estimated accounted for 65% of crypto security incidents in 2025. One simulated attack involves the red team posing as job recruiters to test employees.

Robinhood in Talks with Crypto.com Over Prediction Markets: WSJ

Robinhood is reportedly in discussions with Crypto.com to expand its prediction markets offering, according to a Friday Wall Street Journal report. The company behind the popular stock and crypto trading app is in talks with the exchange to place yes-or-no event contracts supplied by Crypto.com. The move came just days after Bernstein analysts raised their price target on Robinhood (HOOD) stock to $160 from $130 per share, based on the company's outlook for prediction markets and tokenized equities. Bernstein estimated that Robinhood's revenue including prediction markets could reach $1.7 billion by 2028. However, prediction market platforms face ongoing legal challenges in the US between state and federal authorities over jurisdiction.

Markets Bitcoin

Bitcoin Falls Under $64K as Surging Bond Yields Boost Rate-Hike Odds

Bitcoin fell more than 1.6% on Friday as its latest price correction accelerated, dipping under the $64,000 mark before a Binance "plunge protection team" reemerged with bid liquidity to avoid a deeper rout. Data from TradingView showed BTC/USD approaching $64,000 as bulls struggled to preserve gains. Trading firm Mosaic Asset Company said rising US Treasury yields were a key driver of the sell-off, noting that the two-year yield at 4.31% sits "well above the Federal Reserve's target range." CME Group's FedWatch Tool showed markets pricing in a 0.25% hike in September as one of two expected increases before the end of 2026. Geopolitical tensions and macroeconomic headwinds weighed on crypto markets as appetite for risk assets faded across the board.

Markets ETFs

Ethereum ETFs Close Week in Red, End Five-Day Inflow Streak

US-listed spot Ethereum exchange-traded funds logged $70.62 million in net outflows on Friday, ending a five-day inflow streak, according to SoSoValue data. Despite the reversal, ETH ETFs still posted $103.9 million in net inflows for the week and extended their weekly inflow streak to three straight weeks. The reversal followed a similar pattern in Bitcoin ETFs, which ended a seven-day inflow streak on Thursday and recorded another $240.08 million in net outflows on Friday. Bitcoin ETFs also extended their net inflow streak to three consecutive weeks, adding $103.90 million during the week. Spot crypto ETF flows have become one of the market's most closely watched gauges of institutional demand for Bitcoin and Ether through traditional investment products.

Mining Bankruptcy

Bitcoin Mining Pool Poolin Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy

Singapore-based Bitcoin mining pool Poolin, once the world's largest mining pool in 2019, has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in a New Jersey court. The filing shows estimated liabilities of $100 million to $500 million, assets of $1 million to $10 million, and 10,001 to 25,000 creditors. Poolin now ranks as the 17th largest mining pool operator by hashrate with just 0.2% market share. The company is seeking court approval to sell two West Texas mining sites — Tarbush and Pyote — to Thor CALAP LLC under a proposed $52 million stalking-horse bid. Bitcoin mining operations face growing financial constraints from rising electricity costs, with many pivoting to AI infrastructure — Hut 8 and IREN recently announced major AI deals totaling over $12 billion.

✦ Fidelity Joins Push for Senate Passage of CLARITY Act ✦

Fidelity Investments, the world's third-largest asset manager with $7.1 trillion in managed assets, called on the US Senate to pass the CLARITY Act on Friday, joining a growing coalition of financial firms and crypto organizations urging lawmakers to advance digital asset market structure legislation. The Crypto Council for Innovation, the Digital Chamber and the Blockchain Association sent a joint letter to Senate leaders urging them to bring the bill to the floor before the August recess. Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong also called for a full Senate floor vote. The CLARITY Act would establish the first comprehensive federal regulatory framework for digital assets in the US. The bill needs 60 votes to pass the Senate, where Republicans hold a 52-47 majority. Updated bill text was released Wednesday, but some Democrats argue the ethics provisions do not go far enough to address corruption concerns.
