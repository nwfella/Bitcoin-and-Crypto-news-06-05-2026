Markets BTC Price Action

Bitcoin Price Sinks to 2-Week Lows as US Stocks Fail to Copy Asia Rebound

Bitcoin fell to its lowest levels in over two weeks on Friday, sliding 3.5% to $62,369 on Bitstamp — a level last seen on July 14 — as US stocks turned red at the open and treaded water into the monthly close, diverging from a major relief bounce in Asia where South Korea's KOSPI ended the day up 17.9%, its largest single-day gain on record.

QCP Capital noted crypto trading activity increased around the KOSPI gyrations, calling it evidence of "the growing relationship between crypto liquidity, regional equity positioning and broader technology-sector sentiment." Both Japan and Korea reportedly intervened in currency markets on Thursday, while Japan's central bank held rates at 1.0%.

Despite the pullback, BTC/USD ended July up 8.5% — its strongest July since 2022. But trader Rekt Capital warned history may repeat: "It's likely price will try to maintain these highs in the early stages of August but history suggests price could rollover just like it did in 2022," noting Bitcoin's 50-month EMA at $65,820 continues to act as resistance after two failed breakouts since mid-June.

Regulation Mining — Russia

Russia Expands Crypto Mining Ban to Moscow Through 2032

Russia has added Moscow, the Moscow Region and parts of Kursk Region to its cryptocurrency mining ban list, with restrictions taking effect Aug. 15, 2026 and running through Dec. 31, 2032. Resolution No. 936, signed by Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin on July 25, amends the December 2024 order and also covers eight municipal districts and the city of Lgov in Kursk — areas bordering Ukraine.

The Moscow Region's energy ministry cited growing electricity demand, noting 65 data centers connected to the grid with a combined capacity of 734 megawatts, including 19 regionally with 233 MW. Earlier restrictions remain in parts of Buryatia and Zabaykalsky Krai through March 2031.

Trump Media Sells Another 2,628 BTC, Holdings Fall to 4,261 BTC

Trump Media & Technology Group, the company behind Truth Social, sold another 2,628 Bitcoin worth about $165 million through transfers to Crypto.com, according to blockchain analytics platform Lookonchain, which said Trump Media bought 11,542 BTC at an average price of $118,522 before beginning to sell portions of its holdings seven months ago.

The transactions came amid Trump Media launching its new paid data service "Truth API," providing faster access to Truth Social posts from President Trump — reportedly costing as much as $100,000 per month for a "direct, licensed, real-time feed of the platform's most market-moving Truths."

Industry BNB Chain

BNB Chain Pursues Legal Action After Ex-Employee's Memecoin Launch

BNB Chain, the blockchain ecosystem behind the BNB cryptocurrency, said it is pursuing legal action after a former employee allegedly used unauthorized access to a wallet — originally created for a video tutorial on launching tokens — to create a new token after leaving the company.

Lookonchain alleged the former employee deployed a meme token called Asteroid Shiba (ASTEROID) and used four newly created wallets to buy 796.7 million tokens, representing 79.67% of the total supply, later selling 718.8 million for 1,103 BNB (~$638,000). "BNB Chain did not create, authorize, promote or participate in the creation of this token," the ecosystem said. Binance founder CZ shared the statement, calling the ex-employee "basically a scammer" and advising users to "Stay SAFU."

✦ Analysis: The 100x Obsession — Why Great Projects Keep Losing to Great Stories ✦

Crypto's fundamentals have never been stronger, yet degens keep chasing hot new narratives — and behavioral finance pioneer Meir Statman says that's no accident: "Investments change fast; human nature and human aspirations stay constant." A recent MarketWise study found a sealed Pokémon card box outperformed Bitcoin on a $10,000 investment between January 2021 and April 2026, while limited-edition sneakers nearly matched Dogecoin's returns — evidence that investors are "buying a lottery ticket to a life-changing outcome," not the best asset.

Statman argues investors mentally split wealth into a "not-poor" layer and a "be-rich" layer, making concentrated bets rational even when statistically risky. Aave's token trades ~85% below its 2021 peak even as its TVL exceeds $14 billion; revenue-generating protocols struggle to compete with the possibility, however remote, of a 100x moonshot. "The real skill isn't identifying exciting investments, it's recognizing when optimism has already been priced in." Meanwhile institutions play a different game entirely — underwriting risk-adjusted performance, liquidity and operational resilience "long before they look at upside potential," says Talos' Samar Sen. The next Bitcoin isn't really the point; the hunt for transformation is.

Minnesota Crypto ATM Ban Goes Into Effect After Reported $1M Losses

Research Stablecoins

Bank of Italy Finds No Consistent Cost Advantage for Stablecoin Remittances

A Bank of Italy study of 200 USDC remittances across 10 payment corridors linking Italy with Brazil, Argentina, Japan, the UAE and South Africa found stablecoin transfers offered no systematic cost or speed advantage over traditional rails — because fiat on- and off-ramp frictions absorb most of the expense. End-to-end costs ranged from 0.3% to nearly 9% depending on corridor. Against the World Bank's 6.65% global average, stablecoins were cheaper in most corridors — though less expensive than Wise in only three of seven comparable routes.
