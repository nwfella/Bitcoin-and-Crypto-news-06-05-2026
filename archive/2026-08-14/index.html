Regulation Washington — The Pulled Agenda

SEC Cancels Key Crypto Regulatory Meeting at the Last Minute

The US Securities and Exchange Commission canceled an open meeting scheduled for Friday that was expected to consider new cryptocurrency rules. The commission had been expected to weigh whether to propose a "tailored offering regime for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets" — a step toward rulemaking that industry observers have awaited through a year of enforcement-first policy.

The SEC told Reuters the meeting was moved due to "an unforeseen scheduling issue." The cancellation lands as the Senate leaves for its August recess without voting on the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, which would give financial regulators a comprehensive framework for overseeing crypto. SEC Chair Paul Atkins told CNBC in late July that the agency was "ready, willing, and able to come out with rules" on digital assets if the Senate failed to pass the CLARITY Act.

Miners The Energy Pivot

Public Bitcoin Miners Cut Hashrate 13.4% as AI Infrastructure Revenue Grows

Publicly traded Bitcoin miners are shedding mining capacity faster than the network overall. BlocksBridge Consulting's Miner Weekly reported realized hashrate among public miners fell from 368.3 EH/s in Q4 2025 to 319 EH/s in Q2 2026 — and 21.2% excluding Bitdeer — versus a 10.6% decline for the Bitcoin network's average hashrate over the same period.

The divergence signals a broader shift of electricity and infrastructure toward data centers and high-performance computing. Core Scientific generated $136.7 million in colocation revenue in Q2 versus just $27.5 million from Bitcoin mining, and Core Scientific and TeraWulf now earn the majority of their revenue from non-mining activities, while Riot Platforms and Bitdeer remain earlier in the transition.

Institutions Tel Aviv — The New On-Ramp

Israel's Largest Bank Taps Galaxy to Offer Bitcoin, Ether, Solana Trading

Israel's Bank Leumi has partnered with Galaxy Digital to let customers trade Bitcoin (BTC), Ether (ETH) and Solana (SOL) through its investment platform, with the service expected to launch in early 2027. Customers of Leumi and Pepper, its mobile banking arm, will buy, hold and sell the three cryptocurrencies through a dedicated section of the Leumi Trade app.

Leumi will use GalaxyOne Institutional for trading and related services, while Galaxy's custody infrastructure platform, formerly known as GK8, will support the bank's digital asset infrastructure. The deal comes after Galaxy reported an $85 million net loss in Q2, largely attributed to declining digital asset prices, even as its digital assets business generated $66 million in adjusted revenue.

Law Seoul — The Verdict

Delio CEO Sentenced to 15 Years in Prison on Crypto Fraud in South Korea

A South Korean judge sentenced Delio CEO Jeong Sang-ho to 15 years in prison after he was found guilty of defrauding users out of about $50 million in crypto. The Seoul Southern District Court convicted Jeong on charges related to embezzlement and use of a false trust document, saying he "falsely obtained a virtual asset trading license and defrauded victims of approximately 70 billion won [$49.3 million]."

Launched in 2022, Delio marketed itself as a "digital asset bank" offering high interest on crypto deposits, froze customer withdrawals in June 2023 and declared bankruptcy in November 2024. The ruling is the latest crypto fraud case among South Korean exchanges — a list that includes the collapse of Terraform Labs in May 2022.

✦ Feature: Ethereum's Post-Quantum Pivot ✦

The Ethereum Foundation is abandoning the Poseidon hash function in its planned post-quantum architecture, according to researcher Justin Drake, turning instead to established alternatives such as SHA or BLAKE. Poseidon had been considered for future post-quantum systems like leanVM — which would help Ethereum efficiently verify large volumes of blockchain activity — but advances in SNARKs now mean conventional hashes can perform efficiently enough to replace it.

Drake said a production-ready leanVM is targeted for 2027, followed by deployments across Ethereum's consensus, data and execution layers in 2028. Eigen Labs founder Sreeram Kannan said established hash-based systems have fewer known avenues of attack and can be deployed faster because they have already undergone years of scrutiny — adding that joint work with the foundation and zero-knowledge firm Succinct had increased proving speeds 2.5 times.
