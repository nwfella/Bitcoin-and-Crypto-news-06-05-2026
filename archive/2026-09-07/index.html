Security Sidechain Standoff

Bitcoin Sidechain Liquid Pauses After Purported 'White Hats' Withdraw $320M in BTC

Bitcoin sidechain Liquid has paused operations after actors claiming to be white-hat hackers withdrew about 4,000 Bitcoin — worth $320 million and roughly 95% of the ~4,200 BTC federation wallet that backs L-BTC — from its bridge on Sunday. Bridge nodes were disabled to prevent new transactions, and exchanges halted or prepared to halt L-BTC deposits and withdrawals. Other assets issued on Liquid, including USDT, DePix and real-world assets, were unaffected, the network said.

Blockstream, Liquid's technology provider, contacted the actors through signed onchain messages — a dialogue that began at 11:30 am Pacific time and unfolded in public transaction notes, according to a timeline compiled by Jan3 CEO and former Blockstream chief strategy officer Samson Mow. The actors have demanded that the vulnerability be fixed and every node updated before they return most of the Bitcoin; they also sent encrypted technical details to Blockstream. At the time of writing, the funds — about 3,998.5 BTC — had not been returned.

SideSwap said the withdrawal passed through its peg-out service as a customer order using its Peg-out Authorization Key, but that the key was not compromised — blaming instead a bug in Elements, the open-source software underpinning Liquid. Galaxy Digital research head Alex Thorn said the exchange of encrypted technical details suggests a genuine effort to remediate, while the network's federation races to patch the vulnerability that froze the chain's largest wallet.

Networks The Long Goodbye

Harmony Proposes Shutting Down Its Layer 1, Migrating ONE to Ethereum

Ethereum-compatible layer-1 network Harmony has proposed sunsetting its blockchain and migrating its native ONE token to Ethereum — seven years after launching its mainnet and less than four weeks after an exploit created forged ONE tokens and forced it to plan a rollback of more than 109,000 transactions. Under the Sunday proposal, Harmony would take a final network snapshot, issue ERC-20 ONE tokens on Ethereum, and migrate exchange listings, with no claims required of holders.

Validators would be offered options to stop their nodes, continue as governors, or join Harmony's new AI-video initiative, with a $1.372 million pool set aside to compensate those who stop on time, retain their stakes and agree to serve as governors. The snapshot would cover wallets, staking delegations, validator rewards, smart contracts and centralized exchange balances — but Harmony warned that multisig safes, liquidity pools and onchain applications cannot be migrated, urging users to exit all smart contracts before Sept. 10, when validators may begin shutting down.

The proposal is non-binding and does not specify a final block, but it marks a striking turn: Harmony's August plans to revert the chain to an Aug. 11 checkpoint — discarding 109,126 regular transactions and 315 staking transactions after investigators traced nearly all of the ~4 billion forged tokens to wallets and service boundaries — have given way to the question of whether the network should survive at all.

✦ Feature: Markets — Zcash's Privacy Renaissance ✦

Zcash climbed to its highest price since 2016 over the weekend, pushing the privacy-focused cryptocurrency's market capitalization above $20 billion — with Grayscale's research desk arguing that AI could make shielded money a "must have."

ZEC touched $1,249.28 before retreating to roughly $1,190; at press time the token changed hands near $1,165 per CoinGecko, up about 45% over the past week and 138% over 30 days — a rally that leaves the coin below its launch-era all-time high of $3,191.93, set on Oct. 28, 2016, when only a small supply of tokens existed. The move has been turbocharged by Grayscale's conversion of its Zcash Trust into a spot ETF: the product, trading under the ticker ZCSH on NYSE Arca since Aug. 25, closed Friday at $83.77 with $463.2 million in assets under management.

The thesis is partly about artificial intelligence. Grayscale head of research Zach Pandl argued in an Aug. 31 analysis that AI could increase demand for financial privacy by making it easier to link public blockchain transactions to users' identities, positioning Zcash's shielded, zero-knowledge transactions as a premium feature. "For users that prioritize privacy, this could become a 'must have' feature," he wrote — as Zcash's rally hands privacy advocates their loudest moment in a decade.

Markets The $3.8 Billion Quarter-Hour

Bitcoin ETF Inflows Hit $3.8B in Strongest Three-Week Stretch of 2026

US-listed spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded their strongest three-week inflow stretch of 2026, attracting $986.9 million in the week ending Friday and bringing net inflows over the past three weeks to $3.8 billion, according to SoSoValue. Total net assets across the funds stood at $101.3 billion on Friday after briefly rising to $103.3 billion a day earlier, with cumulative net inflows reaching $55.6 billion — a sharp turnaround from the heavy outflows that marked the year's first half, though year-to-date flows remain roughly $1 billion negative.

Friday's $174.6 million intake was a comedown from Thursday's near-record ~$731 million — the cohort's highest single-day tally since January. BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) drew $117.4 million on Friday, about 67% of the day's total, with Fidelity's FBTC adding $57.2 million and no other fund recording net flows. The demand contrast is stark: week over week, Bitcoin ETF inflows rose about 7% while spot Ether ETF inflows fell 74% to $218.4 million and XRP ETF inflows slid 83% to $19 million, ending streaks that had run 12 and 11 sessions respectively.

Despite the cooldown, Ether and XRP funds remain in positive territory for the year — about $863 million and $515 million in net inflows respectively — even as Bitcoin, trading near $79,700 at Friday's close, absorbed the surprise nonfarm payrolls print that briefly knocked it below $79,000.

Treasuries Paris Accumulates

Capital B Adds 376 Bitcoin in $29M Purchase, Holdings Reach 3,521 BTC

French Bitcoin treasury company Capital B has acquired 376 Bitcoin for 25.3 million euros ($29.5 million) — an average of €67,182 per coin — lifting its holdings to 3,521 BTC, the firm announced Monday. The purchase was funded by roughly 30.1 million euros ($35 million) in capital raises, including a private placement backed by investor and Blockstream CEO Adam Back and asset manager TOBAM, with Swissquote Bank Europe executing the trade and Taurus providing custody.

The acquisition — Capital B's largest since September 2025, when it bought 551 BTC — moves the firm to 25th among publicly traded companies by Bitcoin holdings, according to BitcoinTreasuries.net, and puts it 15 BTC ahead of Sweden's H100 Group, though both remain behind Germany's Bitcoin Group SE at 3,605 BTC. Capital B has now spent a total of 309.4 million euros building its treasury at an average cost of €87,878 per BTC, and keeps an additional 61 BTC for operational purposes, separate from its reserve.

The buying spree comes even as some peers retreat — K Wave Media and Sequans Communications have moved to unwind their Bitcoin treasuries — while the biggest holders press on: Metaplanet added 2,823 BTC in the second quarter to reach 43,000, and Strategy resumed buying in August after a two-month pause, acquiring 4,603 BTC for $370 million to bring its hoard to 845,050 BTC.

Security The Third Wave Moves

Coldcard Third-Wave Attacker Moves 45% of Stolen Bitcoin

The exploiter behind the third wave of the Coldcard wallet hack has moved about 45% of their Bitcoin haul, routing funds through THORChain or into CoinJoin transactions, according to a Monday update from Galaxy Research. The attacker began moving funds to Ethereum on Sept. 2 and has now emptied the 11 largest of 293 two-of-two multisignature vaults built to hold victims' coins — the movements even helped Galaxy identify a previously unknown vault that likely holds another victim's funds. Across all waves of the exploit, roughly 82% of the stolen Bitcoin remains in the original attacker-controlled addresses. The Coldcard exploit ranks as 2026's third-largest, behind the $293 million Kelp DAO and $280 million Drift hacks.

Solana Social Trading Surge

Fomo Overtakes Pump.fun in Daily Revenue on Solana

Social trading platform Fomo generated $1.76 million in daily revenue on Friday, edging past memecoin launchpad Pump.fun's $1.1 million, according to DefiLlama — though Pump.fun still dominates over longer horizons, with more than $57 million in revenue over the past 30 days versus $17.6 million for Fomo. Fomo says more than 68,000 users made their first crypto purchase on the app via Apple Pay — about $25 million in volume — and it expanded into perpetual futures powered by Hyperliquid for non-US users in June.

Markets Labor Day Lull

Bitcoin Grinds Lower as $80K Hangs in the Balance on Holiday Liquidity

Bitcoin drifted nearly 2% below $80,000 on Monday, erasing the weekend's gains after its first weekly close above $80,000 since early May, as US markets sat closed for the Labor Day holiday. Thinner order books raised the odds of liquidity hunts: CoinGlass showed $178 million in cross-crypto liquidations over 24 hours, evenly split between longs and shorts, with liquidity clustered at $80,500 and $78,800. Trading firm QCP Capital called the tape "a market waiting for clarity rather than pricing in strong directional views," with US inflation data Thursday and Friday the likely catalyst for a breakout — while Bitget's Ryan Lee marveled at Bitcoin's "resilience" in absorbing last week's shock jobs print, suggesting investors no longer treat a potential Fed hike as the only driver.
