Aviva Investors Launches Tokenized Fund on XRP Ledger After Irish Approval

Aviva Investors, the London-based asset management arm of Aviva Plc, has launched a tokenized share class of its US Dollar Liquidity Fund on the XRP Ledger after receiving approval from the Central Bank of Ireland. The tokenized share class will be available to eligible investors with digital wallets, while the fund's underlying assets remain custodied by BNY Mellon. Komainu will provide digital asset custody and Licuido will supply the tokenization infrastructure. The fund invests in high-grade, short-term US dollar-denominated debt securities issued by governments, banks and corporations. The launch follows Aviva's partnership with Ripple and joins a growing list of tokenized investment products from BlackRock, Franklin Templeton and Apollo on public blockchains.

Robinhood Posts Record Quarter, But Crypto Revenue Slides 38%

Online brokerage Robinhood posted record second-quarter revenue of $1.31 billion, up 32% year-over-year, with net income rising 48% to $573 million. However, cryptocurrency transaction revenue fell 38% to $100 million from about $160 million a year earlier. The company recorded $40 billion in crypto trading volume during the quarter — $18 billion on the Robinhood app and $22 billion from Bitstamp. Robinhood continued expanding its digital asset business, launching the Robinhood Chain mainnet, introducing tokenized US stocks in over 120 countries, and debuting its first decentralized lending product, Robinhood Earn.

Industry AI Infrastructure

Hyperscale Data Sells 100 BTC to Fund Michigan AI Data Center

Bitcoin miner Hyperscale Data has sold about 100 Bitcoin and secured a Bitcoin-backed credit facility at variable interest rates of about 4.5% to 5.0% to help finance construction of its Michigan AI data center. The campus is being developed to support a previously announced master services agreement with an unnamed AI infrastructure provider, initially covering about 20 MW of AI compute capacity. The 10-year agreement includes two optional five-year extensions and is expected to generate more than $1.2 billion in revenue if fully exercised. The company still holds about 1,006 BTC, making it the 44th-largest public corporate Bitcoin holder.

Regulation MiCA

Hungary Repeals Crypto Checks as First MiCA License Is Granted

Hungary is rolling back strict crypto rules as CoinCash prepares to resume services after receiving authorization under the European Union's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation. The Hungarian Parliament voted to repeal the country's crypto validator requirement, removing mandatory third-party approval for certain crypto transactions. Finance Minister Kármán András said the government removed the validation requirement after the previous rules disrupted Hungary's crypto market, prompting some service providers to halt operations. "The market is now showing signs of recovery," he wrote, signaling a regulatory pivot toward MiCA-aligned frameworks.

Luno Cuts 20% of Staff as Crypto Layoffs Spread Across 12 Firms in July

PCE Inflation Breaks Uptrend — But Remains Nearly Double the Fed's 2% Target

The June print of the US Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index came in at 3.7% year-on-year, in line with market expectations and breaking an uptrend that had pushed May's reading to 4.1% — the highest in three years. While the first month-on-month decline since 2020 was welcomed by markets, the Kobeissi Letter noted it was still the second-highest result since October 2024. "US inflation continues to run at nearly double the Fed's 2.0% target," it said. Johns Hopkins economist Steve Hanke described inflation as "the genie the Fed just can't put back in the bottle." The Federal Reserve left interest rates unchanged at its latest meeting, with an emerging split among FOMC members. Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan predicted that future interest-rate announcements would have less impact on BTC price performance, citing smaller expected rate changes under new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh.
