ETF Flows The $3.5B Month

Bitcoin ETFs Cap Best Month of 2026 as August Inflows Hit $3.52 Billion

US-listed spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds capped their best month of 2026 alongside Bitcoin's biggest monthly gain since November 2024. Funds attracted $3.52 billion in net inflows during August — a sharp leap from July's $172 million, per SoSoValue — while Bitcoin rose about 25% on the month, its strongest performance since a 37.29% rally in November 2024, according to CoinGlass.

The August momentum has given way to a weaker start to September: ETF flows turned negative as the new month opened, and Bitcoin briefly fell below $77,000 before stabilizing near $77,200 in Wednesday trading. The monthly tally lands as corporate treasuries pile into the asset — Strategy, Strive, Bitmine and Metaplanet all made multi-hundred-million-dollar moves in the past week.

Policy The G20 Accord

G20 Members Tout 'Clear Pathways' for Digital Asset Innovation

The Group of 20 — chaired this year by the United States — said all member countries agreed with statements recognizing the "transformative role" of digital asset innovation, in a speech released by the US Treasury Department on Tuesday. The body committed to "advancing responsible and effective regulatory and supervisory frameworks" that preserve financial stability and "establish clear pathways for sound digital financial and digital assets innovation."

The G20 also called for better cross-border payments and said it was awaiting Financial Stability Board results on global stablecoin arrangements and data sources. The statements follow a wave of national frameworks — from the EU's MiCA to the US GENIUS Act — and landed the same week 21 global banks unveiled their own dollar-stablecoin venture targeting 2027.

Infrastructure The Excess Rewards

Core DAO Plans Emergency Hard Fork Over Excess Validator Rewards

Core DAO is coordinating an emergency hard fork after validators claimed more CORE rewards than the blockchain intended to issue. In an update, the project said the incident is contained and that "malicious validators" can no longer draw excess rewards — while stressing the fork will be a forward upgrade that neither rolls back the network nor reverses confirmed transactions.

Exchanges moved quickly: Coinbase and four other platforms restricted CORE transfers while the situation was assessed. The episode is the second issuance-related incident to roil a proof-of-stake chain this week, after Core's validator reward overrun surfaced just as Solana's validators voted to slash their own issuance schedule.

Treasuries The Coinbase Move

Metaplanet Moves $377M in Bitcoin to Coinbase, Fueling Sale Speculation

Metaplanet, the fourth-largest corporate Bitcoin treasury, transferred 4,800 BTC worth nearly $377 million to Coinbase Prime, per Arkham Intelligence data — the latest in a week of deposits that now total 10,270 BTC, including a 2,000 BTC ($155.5M) move two days ago and a 3,331 BTC (~$266M) transfer three days prior. Onchain observers read the pattern as "likely to sell"; others note the exchange may simply be acting as custodian.

Cointelegraph found no conclusive evidence of intent to sell. The company holds 35,102 BTC worth $2.75 billion — a disposal would reduce its stack by more than 29% — making the transfer one of the largest custody moves by a corporate holder since Strategy's record accumulation began.

✦ Feature: The 65-Week Accumulator ✦

Tom Lee's Bitmine Immersion Technologies extended its Ether buying streak to 65 consecutive weeks, adding 53,501 ETH last week as the market rebound lifted its portfolio to more than 5.9 million tokens — roughly $14.8 billion at Sunday's Ether price of $2,511, and about 4.9% of Ethereum's 120.7 million circulating supply. The company is now within striking distance of its stated goal of owning 5% of all Ether.

Chairman Tom Lee says Ether, Bitcoin and Solana have been the three best-performing major assets since June 30 — with ETH leading — arguing the run "sets the stage for institutions to add to their crypto holdings given the substantial outperformance of crypto versus other macro assets in 3Q so far." The accumulation has come at a cost: Bitmine sits on roughly $5.1 billion in unrealized losses on its Ether position, per DropsTab, reflecting purchases made through a downturn that began in the fourth quarter of last year. Its average cost, once a talking point for skeptics, is steadily being diluted lower with every weekly buy.

Governance SGP-0002 Passes

Solana Governance Approves Faster Reduction in SOL Issuance

Solana validators approved a proposal to double the network's annual disinflation rate, cutting future SOL issuance — the chain's first binding governance vote. Final tallies show 67% support, 25.16% against and 7.84% abstaining on 60.7% participation. The measure, SGP-0002 or "Double Disinflation," raises the annual disinflation rate from 15% to 30% while leaving the long-term 1.5% inflation target untouched. Kraken reversed its initial opposition before the close.

Regulation The Travel Rule

Thailand Adopts Crypto Travel Rule With Self-Custody Checks

Thailand's SEC finalized Travel Rule regulations requiring digital asset operators to collect information about the parties in crypto transfers — including transactions touching self-custodial wallets — aligning the kingdom with global AML standards. The rules take effect Feb. 27, 2027, giving businesses nearly six months to build systems for transmitting, receiving and monitoring transaction data. The move extends Bangkok's tightening oversight of digital assets after its March proposal to open retail access to certain overseas crypto derivatives.

Ethereum The Gas Warning

Ethereum Warns Developers Over Glamsterdam Gas Changes

The Ethereum Foundation warned that gas-model changes in the upcoming Glamsterdam upgrade may break wallets, indexers and gas estimators. The Protocol DevOps team said any tool relying on a hardcoded maximum gas limit "will break" and must be updated, urging developers to test on Plataberget, the public testnet launched Aug. 13 that is meant to run for several months. Glamsterdam is scheduled to activate Thursday, ahead of deployments on the Sepolia and Hoodi testnets.

Security The Oracle Wound

Sui DeFi Project Full Sail Shuts Down After Oracle Incident

Full Sail, a DeFi protocol on Sui, is winding down after a suspected compromise of oracle provider Switchboard's infrastructure led to user losses. The project has disabled new deposits and LP reward claims, with regular pools moving to withdrawal-only mode after final security checks and compensation for users its "top priority." Switchboard said it was investigating a potential compromise of its Move-based implementations and had halted its network on Aptos, Sui, IOTA and Movement.
