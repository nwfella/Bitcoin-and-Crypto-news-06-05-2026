Regulation The Wyoming Warning

Gallego Warns Against Rushing CLARITY Act Senate Vote

Democratic Senator Ruben Gallego warned that rushing the CLARITY Act to a Senate vote before lawmakers resolve disputes over ethics and stablecoin yield could set US crypto market structure legislation back. Speaking at the SALT Wyoming Blockchain Symposium on Wednesday, Gallego said the industry should encourage Senate Democrats and Republicans to keep negotiating instead of pushing for an immediate vote: "Don't go for a fast vote. A fast vote gets you a fast result, but I'm not sure it's the result you want."

Gallego said he and Republican Senator Thom Tillis submitted compromise ethics language to the White House before the congressional recess but had not received a point-by-point response — and that sufficiently strong ethics restrictions were necessary to attract Democratic support. The warning complicates the administration's swift-passage push by suggesting a procedural vote could arrive before negotiators have the bipartisan coalition needed to clear the Senate's 60-vote threshold.

L2 Governance The Airdrop Reversal

Optimism Redirects 546.9M OP Airdrop Allocation to Ecosystem Fund

Optimism's governance has approved a proposal to repurpose 546.9 million OP tokens previously reserved for user airdrops into a new Strategic Ecosystem Fund supporting partnerships with chains, protocols and institutions, plus incentives to lift activity and liquidity on OP Mainnet and grow OP Enterprise. OP currently has a market cap of roughly $214 million against a circulating supply of about 2.29 billion tokens.

The decision drew pushback from delegates who argued the tokens had been promised to users and questioned how returns from the fund would be measured; supporters said the allocation would be better spent competing for enterprise deals. Optimism said it has no additional airdrops planned after distributing 269.1 million OP across five rounds, arguing airdrops suited an earlier phase of broad user acquisition rather than its current institutional push.

Mining & AI The Malaysia Compute Deal

Bitdeer Signs $400M AI Cloud Computing Deal for Malaysia Facility

Bitcoin mining company Bitdeer's AI division signed a five-year customer deal covering about 50% of the capacity of its A102 Malaysia facility before energization. The agreement with an undisclosed customer of "high credit quality" is expected to bring approximately $400 million in total revenue, with revenue and associated costs beginning in the first quarter of 2027 when services commence.

Bitdeer AI is targeting 350 megawatts of AI cloud data center capacity by Q1 2028, building on an earlier August deal: a 16-year lease valued at $4.7 billion for 121 megawatts of AI computing capacity in Norway. Bitdeer is among the miners — alongside MARA Holdings, TeraWulf, Hut 8 and IREN — that have expanded into AI infrastructure and high-performance computing to diversify revenue streams.

CBDC Eight More Operators

Digital Yuan Operator Count Hits 30 as China Adds 8 Banks

China's central bank has added eight banks to its digital yuan network, bringing the number of e-CNY operating institutions to 30. The People's Bank of China added Ping An Bank, Hengfeng Bank, China Bohai Bank, Bank of Shanghai, Bank of Hangzhou, Huishang Bank, Bank of Changsha and Guangxi Beibu Gulf Bank, per a Xinhua report — the banks will connect to the PBOC's digital yuan system and begin services after completing operational and technical preparations.

The expansion follows 12 additions in April, when city commercial banks joined the operator list; before this year, only 10 banks were authorized operators. Merchants Union Consumer Finance chief researcher Dong Ximiao said new operators could fill gaps in services for regional small and medium-sized enterprises and cross-border trade.

✦ Feature: Fewer Winners in the Next Altseason ✦

Crypto's next altcoin season may produce fewer winners as institutions concentrate their activity in a narrower group of assets, according to market maker Wintermute. In its OTC flow report for the first half of 2026, institutional counterparties generated 72% of spot flow across all tokens on its OTC desk — the highest share on record, up from 61% in the second half of 2025 and 59% a year earlier.

The concentration is striking: between the first half of 2024 and the first half of 2026, the number of unique tokens traded by Wintermute's institutional counterparties grew just 24%, compared with 76% among retail clients. With institutional activity concentrated in fewer tokens and fading faster after price surges, the findings suggest future altcoin rallies could become narrower and more selective — liquidity clustering around favored assets while the market's "long tail" cools.

Ethereum The Hash Function Flip

Ethereum Foundation Drops Poseidon From Post-Quantum Plan

The Ethereum Foundation is moving away from the Poseidon hash function in its planned post-quantum architecture, researcher Justin Drake said Thursday, turning to established alternatives such as SHA or BLAKE. Poseidon had been considered for future systems like leanVM, but Drake said advances in SNARKs mean conventional hashes now perform efficiently enough to replace it. A production-ready leanVM is targeted for 2027, with deployments across Ethereum's consensus, data and execution layers in 2028 — dates remain preliminary.

Politics The Poll

63% of Americans Say Trump Family's Crypto Investments Not 'Appropriate': Poll

A Reuters/Ipsos poll of 1,166 people conducted Aug. 14–17 found 63% of respondents said it was not appropriate for President Donald Trump and his family to earn through crypto investments while in office — with 92% of Democrats opposed, though 69% of Republicans called it appropriate. The survey lands after a June Office of Government Ethics report disclosed Trump earned $1.4 billion from crypto-related investments in 2025, including via World Liberty Financial and the Official Trump memecoin; several lawmakers have called for investigations.

Regulation The VASP License

BitGo Korea Secures VASP Registration for Crypto Custody

BitGo Korea has secured a virtual asset service provider registration in South Korea after the Korea Financial Intelligence Unit accepted its local entity's registration, allowing it to offer custody and transfer services to institutional and enterprise clients. The company built the local entity — with security, anti-money laundering and internal control frameworks tailored to Korean requirements — rather than acquiring an existing provider. Ethereum The Rollup Vote

GnosisDAO Approves Gnosis Chain for Ethereum Economic Zone

GnosisDAO approved Gnosis Chain's transition from a standalone layer-1 to a ZK-proven Ethereum Economic Zone rollup. GIP-153 passed with 123,158 GNO in support across 54 voters, exceeding the 75,000 quorum. Under the proposal, Gnosis Chain's validator set would be retired and the network would settle transactions on Ethereum, becoming an L2 that relies on Ethereum's validators. An initial launch is targeted for late 2026 or early 2027, enabling Gnosis-native contracts to call Ethereum and use results in the same transaction — unlocking mainnet assets and liquidity in a consumer-optimized environment.
