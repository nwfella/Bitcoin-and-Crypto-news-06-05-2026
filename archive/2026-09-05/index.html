Europe Warsaw Override Fails

Poland Upholds Crypto Bill Veto as Zondacrypto Scandal Widens

Polish lawmakers have again failed to secure the three-fifths majority needed to overturn President Karol Nawrocki's veto of legislation aimed at strengthening oversight of the country's crypto market. The Sejm, Poland's lower house, voted 241-198 on Friday to override, with three abstentions — falling 25 votes short of the 266 needed. It was the third time Nawrocki, who argues the proposed rules overregulate the industry, has vetoed the package, which would have established Poland's national framework for applying the EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) and placed market oversight under the Polish Financial Supervision Authority (KNF).

The KNF said Friday that the country still lacks a designated authority responsible for supervising the cryptoasset market, despite MiCA already applying across the European Union. The regulatory dispute comes amid a deepening scandal involving defunct exchange Zondacrypto: Prime Minister Donald Tusk has cited witness testimony alleging a 2 million Polish zloty ($550,000) payment arrangement involving a foundation linked to former Justice Minister Zbigniew Ziobro, and an unnamed person who allegedly promised a presidential pardon in exchange for testimony.

Prosecutors are investigating suspected fraud and money laundering connected to Zondacrypto — formerly BitBay, whose founder Sylwester Suszek disappeared in 2022 — with losses estimated at no less than 350 million zloty ($95 million). The exchange's operator, BB Trade Estonia, was declared bankrupt by an Estonian court in August; the first creditors' meeting is scheduled for Sept. 17.

Asia Tokenized Securities Roadmap

South Korea Unveils Three-Phase Tokenized Securities Roadmap

South Korea's Financial Services Commission (FSC) introduced a three-phase roadmap to develop infrastructure for tokenized securities — stocks, bonds and funds alike — ahead of the country's first tokenized-securities framework taking effect on Feb. 4, 2027. Under an update to the Act on Electronic Registration of Stocks and Bonds, tokenized securities will be legally recognized as digitized forms of securities from that date.

Phase one offers legal recognition for tokenized institutional money market funds, bonds, unlisted stocks and fractional investment securities. Phase two would expand tokenization to all publicly offered securities, while phase three aims for onchain payments linked to stablecoins. The FSC plans to propose revisions to subordinate regulations by the end of September and will work with the Korea Securities Depository to build the underlying infrastructure.

The roadmap follows a run of Korean forays into digital finance: in May the FSC said it would fold tokenized assets into the capital markets framework in 2027, and in April the finance ministry announced a pilot using tokenized deposits for government operational spending, with a full rollout slated for the fourth quarter of 2026.

Real-World Assets Mortgages on Injective

Pineapple Financial Puts $1B in Mortgage Records on Injective

Pineapple Financial has moved more than $1 billion in residential mortgage records onto Injective, the layer-1 blockchain focused on financial applications, as part of a broader effort to migrate its historical loan portfolio onchain. Pineapple plans to eventually migrate more than 29,000 funded mortgages worth over $10 billion, each represented by an onchain record tied to the underlying loan file rather than repackaged as a new mortgage security.

The records carry more than 500 data points designed to support verification, audit trails and risk analysis; the migration dashboard now shows 2,079 mortgage records, up from 1,259 at the initiative's December 2025 launch. PAPL0, which tracks the records onchain, has an asset market cap of about $1.1 billion, up 48.2% over nine months, according to Token Terminal — though the tokens represent records, not ownership of the loans.

The push is part of Pineapple's broader Injective relationship, including a separate $100 million INJ digital-asset treasury staked with Kraken as primary validator. Tokenized real estate remains a small slice of the RWA market — about $226.5 million in distributed value versus $38.8 billion across tokenized RWAs tracked by RWA.xyz — but property is a growing focus of the onchain movement.

Regulation Dubai MoU

Dubai's VARA, BlackRock-Backed Securitize Sign Tokenization MoU

Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) and BlackRock-backed tokenization platform Securitize have signed a memorandum of understanding to advance tokenization and digital-asset infrastructure across the UAE, establishing a collaborative framework to support regulated tokenization initiatives and foster institutional participation. No specific projects will be announced "at this stage," a VARA spokesperson said — the agreement is a framework, not a technology stack.

Dubai has emerged as one of the "world's most forward-looking jurisdictions for digital asset innovation," said Securitize co-founder and CEO Carlos Domingo, as tokenization moves from "concept to mainstream financial infrastructure." VARA granted its 50th virtual asset service provider license in early July to tokenization platform Tribe Tokenisation FZE.

The MoU lands amid surging demand for tokenized assets: total RWA holders rose 103% in the past 30 days to 3.2 million, while the total value of tokenized assets climbed 2% to $38.5 billion, per RWA.xyz. Securitize ranks as the world's largest tokenization platform with $4.9 billion in tokenized assets under management; Ondo Finance follows at $3.5 billion.

✦ Feature: Crypto Biz — AI Takes a Back Seat When Bitcoin Climbs ✦

Crypto's August rally put corporate conviction to the test: miners that spent the downturn courting AI investors are once again trading like leveraged bets on Bitcoin, while Strategy and Strive added thousands more BTC to their balance sheets and Bitmine closed in on 5% of all Ether.

Bitcoin's roughly 23% late-August rally lifted beaten-down mining stocks by as much as 67%, reversing a trend that had favored miners pivoting to AI: Canaan, American Bitcoin and Cango gained 41-67%, while CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN — AI-linked names — rose just 15-21%. BlocksBridge Consulting credits the reversal to expanded US Treasury buybacks, renewed regulatory optimism after a White House crypto meeting and a short squeeze that liquidated more than $1.6 billion in positions.

Corporate treasuries moved with the tape. Strive bought 1,800 BTC for roughly $143 million (average price $79,431), lifting its hoard to 23,156 BTC — the fifth-largest public corporate holder — while Strategy resumed buying with 4,603 BTC at $80,318, pushing its holdings above 845,000 BTC after four straight sales since May. Bitmine extended its Ether streak to 65 consecutive weeks, adding 53,501 ETH despite billions in unrealized losses.

Not everyone is concentrating: a consortium of 21 major financial institutions — including Bank of America, Goldman Sachs and Citi — plans a new company to issue stablecoins, targeting a US dollar coin in the first half of 2027 before expanding to other G7 currencies, built to comply with both the US GENIUS Act and the EU's MiCA.

Washington CME Suit Challenged

CFTC Moves to Dismiss CME Lawsuit Over Crypto Perpetual Futures

The CFTC filed a motion to dismiss CME Group's civil suit claiming the regulator's treatment of crypto "futures" as "swaps" ran against Congress — arguing the exchange lacks standing, since "any CFTC-registered exchange can list perpetual futures on digital assets." The agency's filing calls the June lawsuit "much ado about nothing," and a spokesperson previously branded it "lawfare" and "frivolous." CME sued after the commission approved Bitcoin perpetuals for Kalshi and issued a no-action position for similar products on Coinbase; the CFTC has requested an oral hearing on the dismissal.

Enforcement Scam-Center Ledger

FinCEN Ties $13B in Crypto Scams to Overseas Operations

A new FinCEN analysis of more than 33,000 reports filed between September 2023 and December 2025 identified about $12.7 billion in crypto transactions "perpetrated by overseas scam centers" — pig butchering, romance scams and "cryptocurrency confidence schemes" run largely by transnational criminal organizations operating out of Southeast Asian compounds. "Digital asset investment scams pose one of the most significant fraud threats facing Americans today," said acting Under Secretary Gene Lange. The report lands as Myanmar and Cambodia move to criminalize forced labor in the compounds, with penalties up to life in prison.

Hyperscale Data Ends Michigan Bitcoin Mining for AI Tenant

Hyperscale Data has switched off all Bitcoin miners at its Michigan facility following an inspection by an unidentified California-based neocloud provider, preparing the site for an AI data center customer that has contracted 20 megawatts under a 10-year agreement — potentially worth more than $1.2 billion over its maximum 20-year term, with an additional 32 MW option that could push revenue above $3 billion. The pivot has been costly: shares closed at $0.1984, a split-adjusted record low after a one-for-five reverse split, and the company's Bitcoin holdings fell 79% to 215 BTC ($16.7 million) as it funds the conversion from its treasury.
