Markets The Record July

Solana Transactions Hit Record 4.2B as RWA Activity Nears $4B, SOL Rallies 40%

Solana processed a record 4.2 billion transactions in July — up 13.5% from June and roughly 91% higher than December — according to onchain data highlighted by The Kobeissi Letter. The surge preceded a sharp rally that pushed SOL above $100 for the first time since February, with the token gaining about 40% in eight days as a broader crypto recovery took hold.

The activity boom has coincided with a wider expansion of tokenized real-world assets: RWA.xyz data shows distributed RWAs across tracked networks have climbed above $38 billion, with Solana's slice nearing $4 billion, up 11.8% over the past month. The rally accelerated after the US Treasury announced plans to double certain long-dated bond buybacks to at least $4 billion per operation — a move that lowered yields and lifted risk appetite across crypto markets.

Regulation The OFAC Sweep

US Targets Iran's Crypto Sector, Cites Over $100M in Oil-Linked Payments

The US Treasury has expanded its Iran sanctions framework to cover the country's entire digital asset sector, issuing sectoral determinations that let OFAC sanction any foreign individual or company operating in or supporting Iran's crypto industry. The move follows allegations that UAE-based Ukrainian broker Ivan Obukhov processed more than $100 million in crypto payments since 2023 to facilitate oil sales for the IRGC's Quds Force.

The determination — the first sector-wide crypto measure, after earlier actions against exchanges including Zedcex, Nobitex, Shelbit and Aban Tether — "significantly expands" the US ability to sanction participants across the industry, with US-linked property blocked and foreign banks facilitating significant transactions facing account restrictions. Treasury said Iran increasingly uses crypto as a "tool of choice for sanctions evasion."

Regulation The Bangkok Draft

Thailand Moves Closer to Bitcoin, Ether ETFs with Draft Rules

Thailand's SEC has advanced its framework for locally listed spot Bitcoin and Ether ETFs from principles to draft regulations, opening two consultation papers for public comment until Sept. 20. Under the proposals, passive ETFs tracking BTC or ETH — the only two eligible assets in the initial phase — would trade exclusively on the Stock Exchange of Thailand.

Each fund would need to maintain average net exposure of at least 80% of net asset value to its tracked asset over each accounting year, with onshore digital asset custodians remaining the primary providers and qualified foreign custodians permitted "when necessary and appropriate." The draft is the latest step in Bangkok's push to become a global digital asset hub for institutions.

✦ Feature: The Coldcard Trail ✦

Galaxy puts the Coldcard hack's losses at 1,789 BTC — and 87% of it hasn't moved. Galaxy Research has attributed the theft of 1,789.28 Bitcoin from 8,865 addresses to the Coldcard exploit, one of the largest hardware wallet hacks on record. In a Monday post, head of research Alex Thorn said attackers have not spent 1,561 BTC — 87.3% of the attributed losses — with the funds still sitting in collection and holding addresses, including all Bitcoin stolen during the first three attack waves.

The tally draws on 221 victim reports covering 790.72 BTC (44.2% of the total), with a median loss of 1.04 BTC — meaning more than half of reported cases exceeded one coin. Some funds from later attacks have moved through CoinJoin transactions and peel chains, but the largest stolen holdings remain visible onchain, and Galaxy has shared the identified attacker addresses with exchanges, compliance firms and law enforcement in hopes of freezing the funds if they ever reach centralized intermediaries.

Infrastructure The Pasteur Upgrade

BNB Chain Activates Pasteur Hard Fork to Strengthen Bridge Security

BNB Smart Chain activated its Pasteur hard fork on Tuesday, closing bridge verification and validator authorization gaps. The upgrade bundles three BNB Evolution Proposals: BEP-682 rejects duplicate validator entries in cross-chain light-block verification, BEP-695 tightens key-rotation and governance controls, and BEP-675 lets builders submit already-executed blocks — a route that lifted test throughput by ~88%, from 1,237 to 2,324 transactions per second, without changing the 450-millisecond block time.

Ethereum The Gas Reformation

Ethereum Foundation Warns Some Tools May Break with Glamsterdam Upgrade

The Ethereum Foundation warned that wallets, indexers and gas estimators relying on hardcoded gas limits "will break" under Glamsterdam's new gas model. EIP-8037 introduces a separate state-gas dimension: a plain ETH transfer to an existing account still costs 21,000 gas, but sending to a new account incurs an additional state-gas charge. Devs are urged to test on the Plataberget testnet before the fork activates Thursday, ahead of Sepolia and Hoodi.
