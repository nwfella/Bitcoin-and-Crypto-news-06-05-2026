Network The Disinflation Vote

Solana Validators Approve Proposal to Accelerate SOL Disinflation

Solana validators have approved a major change to the network's issuance schedule, doubling its annual disinflation rate from 15% to 30% to slow the pace of new SOL entering circulation. Proposal SGP-0002 passed with 67% support — 25.16% voted against and 7.84% abstained — with nearly 61% of eligible stake participating.

The change keeps Solana's long-term inflation target at 1.5% but is expected to reach it in roughly 2.8 years instead of 5.7, with an estimated 18.9 million fewer SOL issued over the transition. Major validators were divided: Figment opposed, while Helius and Jupiter largely supported; Kraken initially voted against before more than 90% of its voting stake ultimately flipped. The vote lands as US-listed Solana ETFs gain traction, with Bitwise's Solana ETF recently surpassing $1 billion in assets.

Markets The Buyback Vote

ENA Rises 10% After Ethena Foundation Reveals Token Buyback Proposal

The native token of synthetic dollar protocol Ethena posted double-digit gains after the Ethena Foundation unveiled four ecosystem changes, including a fee-switch proposal under which 95% of the net revenue paid to it from core business lines would be used to purchase ENA once the switch is enabled.

Tokenholders have until Sept. 2 to vote; at press time 65 votes representing about 14.4 million ENA in voting power had been cast, all in favor. ENA rose 10.7% in 24 hours and 27% over the past week to trade above $0.17, while Ethena's USDe ranks as the sixth-largest stablecoin with a $4 billion market capitalization on DefiLlama. The foundation also said it had bought locked ENA from certain major seed investors, accelerating the remaining investor unlocks rather than canceling the tokens.

Central Banks The BIS Verdict

BIS Chief Says Stablecoins Not Credible for Payments at Scale

The Bank for International Settlements is renewing its criticism of stablecoins, questioning their credibility as everyday money as governments worldwide build regulatory frameworks around them. BIS General Manager Pablo Hernández de Cos — a candidate to succeed European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde next year — argued that stablecoins do not credibly function as a means of payment at scale.

"Tokenised deposits offer a more direct path to harness tokenisation while preserving the monetary system's foundations," de Cos said. He acknowledged stablecoins could lower government borrowing costs — an argument also made by US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent — but warned the effect could cut both ways: if customers move deposits into stablecoins, banks could face higher funding costs and pass them on to households. He also cited limited interoperability between stablecoin platforms and difficulties consistently applying anti-money-laundering controls.

Politics The 49% Stake

Abu Dhabi Royal Backs Trump-Linked Crypto Bank Venture: WSJ

An Abu Dhabi royal and his co-investors reportedly back the largest stake in the holding company behind World Liberty Financial's proposed US trust bank. Citing people familiar with the matter, the Wall Street Journal reported that Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan's group is behind StringZ Holding RSC, which owns 49% of WLTC Holdings — the vehicle World Liberty says will own the bank.

If it receives final approval, the planned bank would bring the issuance, redemption and custody of World Liberty's USD1 stablecoin under the federally supervised venture. The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency granted World Liberty Trust Company preliminary conditional approval on Aug. 14; the bank cannot begin operating until it satisfies the OCC's pre-opening requirements. Tahnoon previously backed a $500 million purchase of 49% of World Liberty Financial, which drew calls from Democratic senators for hearings.

✦ Feature: The Jackson Hole Signal ✦

Bitcoin saw volatility after Friday's Wall Street open as markets digested US Federal Reserve chair Kevin Warsh's first keynote address at the annual Jackson Hole Symposium. BTC/USD dipped to $78,442 on Bitstamp, down around 1%, as Warsh delivered a cautionary tone on inflation — committing to the Fed's 2% target and dismissing recent lower-than-expected CPI and PCE prints as evidence of a downtrend.

"Forward guidance as a regular practice was adopted by my colleagues and me during the Global Financial Crisis. It was essential at the time, and we introduced it with much fanfare," Warsh said, pushing back on the notion that softer inflation readings signal the Fed's work is done. US stocks avoided losses on the back of his remarks, which included a complimentary view of business performance and AI sector growth, with both the S&P 500 and the tech-heavy Nasdaq holding firm. BTC price action thus continued to gyrate around the $80,000 mark in a narrow intraday range ahead of the August monthly close.

Grayscale Sees Zcash as Potential Bitcoin Challenger

Zcash could emerge as a meaningful challenger to Bitcoin's dominance as the rapid adoption of AI puts a premium on financial privacy, Grayscale head of research Zach Pandl argues. Zcash's ability to shield transaction information could grow increasingly valuable as AI systems become better at analyzing onchain data — "second mover advantages" against BTC's entrenched network effects. Sports The Jersey Deal

Chelsea FC Gets a Stablecoin Sponsor After UK FCA Warning to Clubs

Circle, the issuer behind USDC, will become the latest sponsor for Chelsea Football Club — just months after the UK's financial watchdog warned clubs about "questionable" sponsorship deals. In a Friday announcement, Circle said its name and USDC would appear on jerseys for Chelsea players in the 2026/2027 season. Notably, although Circle said USDC was "issued by certain regulated affiliates," the stablecoin was "not issued or regulated under the laws of the United Kingdom", an FCA spokesperson said.

Ethereum The Gas Warning

Ethereum Foundation Warns Some Tools May Break With Glamsterdam Upgrade

The Ethereum Foundation has warned that wallets, indexers and gas estimators may break because of gas-model changes in the upcoming Glamsterdam upgrade. The EF's Protocol DevOps team said any tool relying on a hardcoded maximum gas limit "will break" and urged developers to test on Plataberget, which launched Aug. 13. EIP-8037 introduces a separate state-gas dimension for operations that create new state, so software assuming 21,000 gas covers every ETH transfer — or using only one gas dimension — needs revisiting. Glamsterdam, scheduled to activate Thursday, also includes enshrined proposer-builder separation and block-level access lists.

Bullish Backs USD.AI With $100M Stablecoin Facility for GPU-Backed Loans

Institutional crypto exchange operator Bullish has provided USD.AI with a $100 million stablecoin-based debt facility to finance loans secured by GPU infrastructure, the companies said. CBDC The 30th Operator

China Adds 8 Banks to Digital Yuan Network as Operator Count Hits 30

China's central bank added eight lenders — including Ping An Bank, Bank of Shanghai and China Bohai Bank — to its digital yuan network, bringing the number of e-CNY operating institutions to 30. The expansion follows the addition of 12 city commercial banks in April; before this year only 10 banks were authorized operators, with the last joining in 2022. Chief researcher Dong Ximiao said the newcomers could fill gaps in services for regional small and medium-sized enterprises and cross-border trade as Beijing keeps broadening the e-CNY's retail footprint.
